# Ground-level Viewpoint Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments

**Year**: 2025  
**Venue**: ICRA`<br> The University of Adelaide  
**arXiv**: [2502.19024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.19024)  

## Overview

（GVNav）依靠调取低视角机器人（比如机器狗，扫地机器人等）在不同高度下获得的视觉信息，来补全当前视角；采用的ScaleVLN，仿真环境成功率为50-60%，机械狗真机成功率28-48%
