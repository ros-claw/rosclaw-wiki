## VLN-Ego Dataset

**VLN-Ego** is a large-scale egocentric dataset designed for training Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) agents within the Habitat 3D Simulator ⚠️ ⚠️ ⚠️ environment. It provides paired egocentric video sequences and corresponding expert action trajectories, enabling agents to learn policy mappings directly from first‑person observations without requiring explicit map knowledge.

### Construction

The dataset was **constructed using** the Habitat 3D Simulator ⚠️ ⚠️ ⚠️, which simulates photorealistic indoor environments from the Matterport3D and Habitat-Matterport 3D (HM3D) scans. Trajectories were generated by an oracle policy that follows navigation instructions in a fully observable setting, then rendered as egocentric RGB frames.

### Capabilities

- **Egocentric video + action pairs**: Each sample contains a sequence of first‑person camera images and the ground‑truth action labels (e.g., forward, turn left/right, stop) taken from the oracle policy.
- **Large‑scale coverage**: Thousands of instruction‑trajectory pairs across hundreds of scenes, suitable for training reactive and sequence‑to‑sequence VLN models.
- **Supports imitation learning**: The direct action supervision makes VLN‑Ego ideal for behavioral cloning and offline reinforcement learning baselines.

### Relationships

- **used_by**: VLN-R1 leverages the VLN‑Ego dataset as a primary training source to learn embodied navigation policies without requiring an online simulator during learning.
