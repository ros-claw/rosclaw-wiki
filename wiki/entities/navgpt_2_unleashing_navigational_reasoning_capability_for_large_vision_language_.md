# Navgpt-2: Unleashing navigational reasoning capability for large vision-language models

**Year**: 2024  
**Venue**: ECCV`<br>University of Adelaid  
**arXiv**: [2407.12366](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.12366?)  
**Code**: [https://github.com/GengzeZhou/NavGPT-2](https://github.com/GengzeZhou/NavGPT-2)  

## Overview

VLM处理图像和指令，然后将VLM获取的视觉Token作为LLM（Frozen）的输入，由LLM生成导航推理，LLM的推理则进一步输入到导航策略网络输出action
