# CL-CoTNav: Closed-Loop Hierarchical Chain-of-Thought for Zero-Shot Object-Goal Navigation with Vision-Language Models

**Year**: 2026  
**Venue**: arXiv`<br>Nanyang Technological University  
**arXiv**: [2504.09000](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.09000)  

## Overview

基于VLM的零样本目标导航框架：结构化推理（将导航任务分解为多轮问答）+闭环反馈机制（引入置信度评分，对每个问答可靠性进行评估），采用LoRA对2B的模型（InternVL2）进行微调，仅在仿真数据下测试，SR为70+%，在新环境下为50+%
