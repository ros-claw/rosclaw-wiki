# Panoramic Occupancy Dataset for Humanoid Robots

## Overview

The **Panoramic Occupancy Dataset for Humanoid Robots** is the first panoramic occupancy dataset specifically curated for the humanoid robots ⚠️ ⚠️ domain. It provides a structured benchmark for training and evaluating occupancy perception systems in humanoid platforms, addressing the prior lack of specialized datasets for this modality.

## Description

The dataset offers a valuable resource for future research in humanoid robot perception, addressing the lack of specialized datasets. It is designed to facilitate development and evaluation of occupancy perception algorithms that can be deployed on bipedal or wheeled humanoid robots.

## Capabilities

- Serves as the first panoramic occupancy dataset specific to humanoid robots.
- Provides a benchmark for research and development in humanoid robot perception.
