# Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is a British-American AI research laboratory owned by Alphabet Inc. Formed from the merger of DeepMind and Google Brain, it is one of the world's leading AI research organizations, known for breakthroughs like AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and large language models.
