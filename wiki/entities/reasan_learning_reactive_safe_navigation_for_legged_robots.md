# REASAN: Learning Reactive Safe Navigation for Legged Robots

**Year**: 2025  
**Venue**: arXiv`<br>University of Groningen  
**arXiv**: [2512.09537](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.09537)  
**Code**: [https://github.com/ASIG-X/REASAN](https://github.com/ASIG-X/REASAN)  

## Overview

三个RL策略网络（运动控制、安全防护和导航）+基于 Transformer 的外感知估计器（LiDAR--->动态障碍物的感知/Ray-based representation）,高达2.5m/s与3 rad/s的速度，Unitree Go2 ，Jetson AGX Orin
