# AI2-THOR

**AI2-THOR** (Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence - The House Of inteRactions) is a high-fidelity, interactive 3D simulation environment designed for training and testing embodied AI agents in household scenarios. It provides photorealistic scenes, physics-based interactions, and a large variety of objects and layouts to support tasks such as object navigation, manipulation, and instruction following.

## Capabilities

- **Household environment simulation**: AI2-THOR offers a rich set of indoor environments (kitchens, living rooms, bedrooms, etc.) with hundreds of interactable objects, enabling the development and evaluation of object navigation algorithms and other embodied reasoning tasks.
