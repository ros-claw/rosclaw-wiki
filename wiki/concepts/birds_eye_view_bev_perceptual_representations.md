# Bird's-eye-view (BEV) Perceptual Representations

A representation approach that projects sensory data into a top-down, bird's-eye-view grid, facilitating downstream planning and navigation tasks.

## Capabilities

- **Structured representation** of the environment from a top-down perspective.
- **Enables reasoning** about spatial layout and navigation costs.

## Relationships
