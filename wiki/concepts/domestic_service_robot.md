# Domestic Service Robot

A **Domestic Service Robot** is an autonomous or semi-autonomous machine designed to perform household tasks, assist with daily activities, and interact naturally with human users in home environments. These robots range from vacuum cleaners and lawn mowers to mobile manipulators capable of fetching items, cleaning surfaces, or providing companionship.

Domestic service robots must operate safely in unstructured human spaces, adapt to variable layouts and lighting, and communicate in ways that are intuitive for non‑expert users. As a result, they often rely on advanced capabilities in perception, navigation, and human-robot interaction.

## Context

The task of interacting naturally with users is particularly suitable for domestic service robots. By combining language understanding with visual perception, these robots can follow spoken commands, answer questions, and navigate to requested objects or locations—enabling truly seamless assistance in the home.
