# LLM

Large Language Models (LLMs) are deep learning models with billions of parameters trained on vast text corpora. They excel at natural language understanding, generation, reasoning, and code synthesis. In embodied AI, LLMs serve as high-level planners, instruction parsers, and reasoning engines.
