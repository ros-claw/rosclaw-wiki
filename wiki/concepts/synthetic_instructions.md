## Overview

**Synthetic Instructions** refer to artificially generated natural-language instructions paired with robot trajectories, designed to augment training data for embodied AI models. These instructions are produced programmatically rather than by human annotators, enabling massive scaling of instruction datasets for tasks such as vision-and-language navigation and manipulation.

## Parameters

- **Scale**: 4.2 million instruction–trajectory pairs  
- **Source generator**: [[Marky]] – a system for generating synthetic instructions from structured environment representations and action sequences.

## Capabilities

- Two orders of magnitude larger than human-annotated datasets (e.g., [[RxR]] or [[R2R]]).  
- Improve language grounding and spatial understanding in [[Imitation Learning]] pipelines.

## Importance

Synthetic instructions enable scaling of training data far beyond what human annotation can provide. In experiments on the [[RxR]] benchmark, models trained with synthetic instructions achieve performance improvements over reinforcement-learning agents, demonstrating that synthetic data can effectively teach language grounding and spatial reasoning.
