# 3D Vision-Language Learning

**3D Vision-Language Learning** is a subfield of [[Embodied AI]] that integrates three-dimensional spatial understanding with natural language instructions. The goal is to enable robots and agents to perceive a 3D scene (e.g., point clouds, voxel grids, or depth images) and ground commands or queries expressed in natural language to specific objects, locations, or actions in that space. This combines techniques from [[3D Vision]] ⚠️ (object detection, scene segmentation, spatial reasoning) and [[Natural Language Processing]] ⚠️ (semantic parsing, reference resolution).

## Capabilities

- Combines 3D spatial understanding with natural language instructions.
- Supports tasks such as visual grounding in 3D, embodied question answering (EQA), and language-guided manipulation.
- Enables cross-modal reasoning: e.g., finding "the red mug on the left shelf" from a language query and a 3D scene representation.
