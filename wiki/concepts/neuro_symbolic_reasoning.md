## Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning

**Neuro-symbolic reasoning** is a paradigm that intertwines neural networks with symbolic reasoning, enabling complex task decomposition and efficient exploration. By combining the pattern recognition strengths of deep learning with the logical, interpretable structure of symbolic AI, this approach improves both interpretability and planning capabilities in intelligent systems.

### Capabilities

- Combine neural networks ⚠️ with symbolic reasoning
- Improve interpretability and planning in autonomous agents

### Usage

This paradigm is used by VL-Nav, a vision-language navigation system that leverages neuro-symbolic reasoning to decompose high-level instructions into executable subgoals and guide exploration efficiently.
