# Vision-Language Models

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) are multimodal AI systems that jointly process and reason over visual and textual information. They enable capabilities such as image captioning, visual question answering, and vision-language navigation. Representative models include CLIP, BLIP, and GPT-4V.
