> Self-improvement through conversation analysis.

Agent-reflect is a tool designed for AI agents to improve their performance by analyzing past conversations. It focuses on self-improvement through systematic reflection on dialogue patterns and outcomes. The system likely uses conversation analysis to identify areas for enhancement in agent behavior. This enables continuous learning and adaptation without external supervision. The approach aligns with meta-cognition in AI systems.
