This repository provides best practices for combining Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) using the Sanity headless CMS. It covers strategies for structuring content to perform well in both traditional search results and AI-driven answer engines like Google's featured snippets. The guide includes tips on metadata, structured data, and content modeling to maximize visibility. It is maintained by the Sanity team as part of their skill resources for developers.
