> 🎖️ 📇 ☁️ 🏠 - Crawl, embed, chunk, search, and retrieve information from datasets through [Trieve](https://trieve.ai)

Trieve is a server implementation tool that crawls, embeds, chunks, searches, and retrieves information from datasets. It integrates with the Trieve platform to provide a complete data pipeline for search and extraction tasks. The tool is designed for server-side use, enabling efficient handling of large-scale data indexing and retrieval.
