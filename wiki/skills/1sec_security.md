> Install, configure, and manage 1-SEC — an open-source, all-in-one cybersecurity platform (16 modules, single binary)

1sec-security is an open-source, all-in-one cybersecurity platform that comes as a single binary with 16 integrated modules. It simplifies installation, configuration, and management of various security tools into one cohesive system. The platform is designed for easy deployment and maintenance, making it suitable for both individuals and organizations. Its modular architecture allows users to enable only the needed components, reducing complexity and resource usage.
