Verdictswarm-mcp is a multi-AI crypto token scanner that uses six independent AI agents to analyze and debate token risk on Solana, Base, and Ethereum networks. The agents work through adversarial consensus to detect suspicious patterns such as rug pulls, honeypots, holder concentration, and wash trading. This MCP server provides a collaborative AI-driven approach for assessing the safety of cryptocurrency tokens in real-time.
