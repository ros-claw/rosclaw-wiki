> 🐍 🏠 ☁️ - HVAC equipment RFQ management for contractors - submit quotes to distributors, track responses, and compare pricing

This MCP server helps HVAC contractors manage request-for-quote (RFQ) processes for equipment. It allows users to submit quotes to distributors, track responses, and compare pricing across different suppliers. The tool aims to streamline procurements workflows in the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning industry.
