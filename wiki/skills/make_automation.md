> Automate Make (Integromat): scenarios, connections, and execution management.

Make Automation is a tool that allows users to automate tasks within Make (formerly Integromat) by programmatically managing scenarios, connections, and executions. It leverages Composio to expose Make's capabilities as an AI-agent tool. This enables seamless integration of Make into larger automation workflows. Users can create, update, trigger, and monitor Make scenarios without manual intervention.
