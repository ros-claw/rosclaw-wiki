> Orchestrate multi-agent teams with defined roles, task lifecycles, handoff protocols, and review workflows.

This tool enables the orchestration of multiple AI agents working together as a team. It defines specific roles for each agent, manages complete task lifecycles from creation to completion, and establishes protocols for handing off tasks between agents. Additionally, it incorporates review workflows to ensure quality control and coordination among team members. The project focuses on structuring agent collaborations to achieve complex goals efficiently.
