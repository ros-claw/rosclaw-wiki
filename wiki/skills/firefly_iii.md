> Manage personal finances via Firefly III API.

Firefly-iii is a command-line tool or wrapper that interacts with the Firefly III personal finance API. It enables users to manage their finances programmatically, including creating transactions and syncing accounts. The project focuses on self-hosted financial management, giving users control over their data. It is designed for automation and integration with budgeting workflows.
