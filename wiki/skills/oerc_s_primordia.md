> 📇 ☁️ - AI agent economic settlement. Verify receipts, emit meters (FREE). Net settlements, credit lines, audit-grade balance sheets (PAID/402).

Primordia is a tool for AI agent economic settlement, enabling agents to verify receipts and emit usage meters for free. Paid features include net settlement capabilities, credit lines, and audit-grade balance sheets. It is designed to provide a financial layer for AI agent interactions, likely using a 402 payment mechanism.
