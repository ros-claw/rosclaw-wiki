SubDownload MCP is a YouTube knowledge base for AI agents. It allows summarizing videos, fetching full transcripts (even for videos without captions using AI ASR), and searching channels and playlists. All retrieved information is saved into a per-user knowledge base for cross-session recall. The service uses OAuth 2.1 with Dynamic Client Registration and is hosted at subdownload.com with free credits on signup.
