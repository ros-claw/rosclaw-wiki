> End-to-end release workflows for TestFlight and App.

asc-release-flow provides automated release workflows for iOS apps targeting TestFlight and the App Store. It simplifies the process of versioning, building, signing, and submitting apps. The tool integrates with App Store Connect APIs and CI systems. It aims to reduce manual steps and errors in mobile app releases.
