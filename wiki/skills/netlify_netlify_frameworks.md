Netlify Frameworks is an open-source tool that automatically detects the JavaScript framework used in a project, such as Gatsby or Next.js. It maps detected frameworks to predefined build configurations, simplifying deployment on Netlify. The project enhances the developer experience by reducing manual setup and ensuring consistent builds. It is maintained by Netlify and listed under Typefully's sponsorship of community skills.
