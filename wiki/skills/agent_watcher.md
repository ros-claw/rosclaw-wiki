> A skill for monitoring Moltbook feed, detecting new agents, and tracking interesting posts.

Agent-watcher is a skill that monitors a feed from Moltbook, a platform for AI agents. It detects new agents as they appear and tracks posts that are considered interesting. This allows users to stay updated on agent activity without manual scanning.
