> Generate market regime reports for crypto perpetuals using Supertrend and ADX indicators.

This project generates market regime reports specifically for cryptocurrency perpetual futures. It uses two technical indicators—Supertrend and ADX (Average Directional Index)—to classify the market as trending or ranging. The reports help traders decide whether to use trend-following or mean-reversion strategies. The tool is tailored to perpetual contracts, which are leverage-based derivatives common in crypto trading.
