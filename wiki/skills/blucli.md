> BluOS CLI (blu) for discovery, playback, grouping.

Blucli is a command-line interface tool for BluOS, enabling users to discover audio devices, control playback, and manage grouping of speakers. It streamlines interaction with BluOS multi-room audio systems through simple terminal commands. The tool focuses on discovery and grouping features for seamless audio management.
