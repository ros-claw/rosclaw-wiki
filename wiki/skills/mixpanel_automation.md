> Automate Mixpanel: events, funnels, cohorts, annotations, and JQL queries.

Mixpanel Automation is a tool that enables automated interactions with Mixpanel's analytics platform. It can programmatically handle events, funnels, cohorts, annotations, and JQL queries. Designed for integration within the Composio ecosystem, it allows AI agents to perform Mixpanel operations without manual intervention. This streamlines data analysis and reporting workflows.
