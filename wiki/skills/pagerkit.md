> Expert guidance on PagerKit, a SwiftUI library for advanced.

PagerKit is a SwiftUI library designed for implementing advanced paging interfaces. It offers components for both horizontal and vertical page scrolling with snapping behavior. The library simplifies the creation of paged content views in SwiftUI, including support for nested paging and custom transitions.
