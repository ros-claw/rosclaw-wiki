> 🐍 ☁️ -  Tracking stablecoin peg integrity across multiple blockchains.

This MCP server monitors the peg integrity of stablecoins across multiple blockchains, providing real-time data on deviations from their target value. It helps users track whether stablecoins maintain their intended 1:1 peg to fiat currencies or other assets. The tool can be used to detect potential depegging events and assess stablecoin risk.
