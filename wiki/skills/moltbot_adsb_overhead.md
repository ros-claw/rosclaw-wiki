> Notify when aircraft are overhead.

moltbot-adsb-overhead is a tool that monitors ADSB (Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast) transmissions to detect aircraft flying nearby. When an aircraft is detected overhead, it sends a notification, likely via a messaging service like Telegram or email. It is typically used with a software-defined radio (SDR) receiver on a Raspberry Pi to decode ADS-B signals. This allows aviation enthusiasts or hobbyists to be alerted when planes are passing by their location. The project provides a simple way to integrate aircraft tracking with personal notification systems.
