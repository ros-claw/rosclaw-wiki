> 🐍 🏠 - Audit URLs for AI crawler readiness — checks robots.txt, llms.txt, JSON-LD schema, and content density with 0-100 AEO scoring.

This tool is a command-line interface that audits a given URL to assess its readiness for AI crawlers. It checks crucial files like robots.txt, llms.txt, and JSON-LD schema, and analyzes content density. It generates a 0-100 AEO (AI Engine Optimization) score to help webmasters and developers optimize their sites for AI-driven search and data extraction. The tool is useful for improving discoverability by AI agents and ensuring efficient crawling.
