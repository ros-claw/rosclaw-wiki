> A benchmark that evaluates AI agents on locating hard-to-find information, stressing search strategy, context management, and retrieval harness design under difficult conditions.

BrowseComp is a benchmark designed to test AI agents on their ability to locate hard-to-find information. It emphasizes search strategy, context management, and retrieval harness design under challenging conditions. The benchmark aims to push the limits of current AI systems in information retrieval tasks.
