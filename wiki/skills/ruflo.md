Ruflo is an orchestration platform designed for deploying and coordinating multi-agent swarms. It features self-learning, autonomous agents, vector-based multi-layered memory, systematic planning, and security guardrails. The project is well-engineered and covers a wide range of features, making it a valuable reference for studying agent orchestration patterns.
