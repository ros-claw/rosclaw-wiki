> A real-world economic benchmark where AI agents complete professional tasks spanning 44 occupations, earning income while managing token costs and economic solvency, making it a direct test of harness efficiency under resource constraints.

ClawWork is a benchmark that tests AI agents by having them perform real-world professional tasks across 44 different occupations. Agents earn income while managing token costs and maintaining economic solvency, simulating a resource-constrained environment. The benchmark directly evaluates the efficiency of agent harnesses under financial pressures. It serves as a practical, economy-driven measure of an AI's ability to handle diverse professional workloads.
