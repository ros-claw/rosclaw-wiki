> Monitor your brand's AI visibility via BotSee API.

BotSee is a tool that allows you to monitor how your brand is perceived or represented in AI systems and outputs. It provides an API to track AI visibility, helping brands understand their presence in AI-generated content. This can be valuable for managing brand reputation in the age of AI. The focus is on enabling automated monitoring through the BotSee API.
