> 🏠 🐧 🍎 ☁️ - Allows you to automate or monitor business operations, sales recorder, POS software, CRM.

This MCP server integrates with point-of-sale (POS) systems, cash registers, and CRM tools to automate and monitor business operations. It allows AI agents to connect to data platforms for sales recording, inventory tracking, and customer management. By leveraging the Model Context Protocol, agents can retrieve or update business data in real time, enabling automated workflows and analytics.
