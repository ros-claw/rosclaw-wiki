> Autonomous deep research powered by Google Gemini.

This project is an autonomous research agent that uses Google's Gemini model to perform in-depth research tasks. It automates the process of searching, gathering, and synthesizing information from various sources. The agent can operate independently, planning and executing complex research workflows without human intervention. It leverages Gemini's advanced reasoning and generation capabilities to produce comprehensive reports.
