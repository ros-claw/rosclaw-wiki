> A Proxmox-native orchestration skill that turns any home lab.

Hydra-evolver is a Proxmox-native orchestration skill designed for home lab environments. It uses Proxmox's APIs to automate the deployment and management of virtual machines and containers. The tool aims to simplify infrastructure-as-code practices for small-scale homelab setups, making them more reproducible and manageable.
