> 📇 🏠 - Update, create, delete content, content-models and assets in your Contentful Space

This MCP server allows AI agents to manage a Contentful space by updating, creating, and deleting content, content models, and assets. It integrates with the Model Context Protocol to bridge Contentful's CMS with AI-powered tools like Claude Desktop. Users can perform content management tasks through natural language or programmatic commands, enabling automated workflows for content operations. The tool streamlines the process of maintaining and manipulating Contentful data without manual intervention.
