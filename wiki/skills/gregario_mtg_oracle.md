> [![gregario/mtg-oracle MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/gregario/mtg-oracle/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/gregario/mtg-oracle) 📇 🏠 - Magic: The Gathering card search, rules lookup, deck analysis, price data, and Commander intelligence. 14 tools, Scryfall + Academy Ruins + Commander Spellbook data.

The mtg-oracle MCP server integrates Magic: The Gathering data into AI agents, providing 14 tools for card search, rules lookup, deck analysis, price data, and Commander intelligence. It pulls from three major data sources: Scryfall for comprehensive card information, Academy Ruins for rules references, and Commander Spellbook for combo detection. This enables AI assistants to answer complex queries about cards, deck building, and game mechanics in real-time. The server simplifies MTG-related tasks for players and developers within MCP-compatible applications.
