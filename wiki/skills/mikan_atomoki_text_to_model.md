> [![text-to-model MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/mikan-atomoki/text-to-model/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/mikan-atomoki/text-to-model) 🐍 🏠 🪟 🍎 - Turn natural language into 3D models in Fusion 360. 64 CAD tools including sketches, extrudes, fillets, and JIS standard parts.

This project provides an MCP server that translates natural language descriptions into 3D models within Autodesk Fusion 360. It offers 64 CAD tools such as sketches, extrusions, fillets, and JIS standard parts, enabling users to design by simply describing what they want. The server is listed under Art & Culture and is available for multiple platforms including Python, Windows, macOS, and Homebrew. It integrates with Fusion 360 to automate model creation, making 3D design more accessible through language.
