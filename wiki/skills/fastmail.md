> Manages Fastmail email and calendar via JMAP and CalDAV APIs.

Fastmail is a project that provides management of Fastmail email and calendar services. It uses the JMAP protocol for email handling and the CalDAV protocol for calendar synchronization. This tool likely offers a command-line interface or programmatic access to automate tasks such as sending emails, organizing messages, and managing calendar events. It integrates with Fastmail's APIs to enable efficient interaction with the user's Fastmail account.
