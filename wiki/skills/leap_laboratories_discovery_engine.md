Data goes in, validated insights come out. Free for public data.

Discovery Engine is a tool for superhuman exploratory data analysis that automatically uncovers feature interactions and subgroup effects often missed by LLMs and manual methods. It outputs validated insights with statistical measures like p-values and effect sizes, plus literature citations. The tool is free to use for public data and operates as an MCP server, integrating with AI-agent workflows.
