> Analyzes meeting transcripts to uncover behavioral patterns including conflict avoidance, speaking ratios, filler words, and leadership style.

Meeting Insights Analyzer is a tool that processes meeting transcripts to detect behavioral patterns such as conflict avoidance, speaking ratios, filler words, and leadership style. It aims to improve meeting effectiveness and team communication by providing actionable insights. The analyzer helps teams identify areas for improvement in collaboration and decision-making processes.
