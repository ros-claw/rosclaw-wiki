> Recursive research up to PhD level across any domain (science, tech, business, arts, humanities) with source tiering, WDM + Munger inversion for autonomous decisions, and disk checkpointing to survive context compaction.

Recursive-research is a method for conducting autonomous, in-depth research across various domains, enabling PhD-level analysis. It uses source tiering to prioritize information quality and incorporates WDM (Weighted Decision Matrix) and Munger inversion for independent decision-making. Disk checkpointing allows saving progress to survive context losses. This tool can handle science, tech, business, arts, and humanities.
