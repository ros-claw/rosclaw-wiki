Find prospects posting about problems your product solves, send personalized DMs at scale via your own Reddit account or a managed bot network of hundreds of accounts, and manage a full outreach CRM — all without leaving your AI client.

This MCP server connects AI agents to Signal Found's Reddit outreach network, enabling automated prospect identification and personalized direct messaging at scale. It allows users to manage outreach campaigns through their own Reddit accounts or a managed bot network, with a built-in CRM for tracking interactions. The tool integrates seamlessly with AI clients to streamline social media lead generation.
