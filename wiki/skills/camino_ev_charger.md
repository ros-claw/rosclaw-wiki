> Find EV charging stations along a route or near a destination using Camino AI's location intelligence.

This project helps users find electric vehicle charging stations along a planned route or near a specific destination. It leverages Camino AI's location intelligence to provide optimized charging stops. The tool aims to reduce range anxiety for EV drivers by integrating charging station data with navigation. It is particularly useful for long-distance travel planning and last-mile charging needs.
