> AI Agent self-evolution engine that enables agents to learn from experience, detect problems, extract insights.

Agent-evolver is a self-evolution engine for AI agents, allowing them to learn from their own experiences. It can detect problems automatically and extract insights from those experiences. The goal is to help agents improve their performance over time without manual intervention.
