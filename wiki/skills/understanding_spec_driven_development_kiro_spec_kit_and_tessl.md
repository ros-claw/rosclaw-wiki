> Thoughtworks on why strong specs make AI-assisted software delivery more dependable.

The article from Thoughtworks explains how using strong specifications can enhance the reliability of AI-assisted software delivery. It introduces tools like Kiro, spec-kit, and Tessl to support spec-driven development. The approach aims to ensure that AI agents generate code that meets human intent and project requirements. By focusing on clear specs, teams can reduce ambiguity and improve outcomes in AI-powered workflows.
