> A benchmark for general AI assistants that is often used to compare harness-level choices around tools, planning, verification, and long-horizon autonomy.

GAIA is a benchmark designed to evaluate general AI assistants. It is commonly used to compare different design choices in AI agent harnesses, particularly regarding tools, planning, verification, and long-horizon autonomy. The benchmark helps assess how well AI systems can handle complex, multi-step tasks that require integration of various capabilities. By providing a standardized test, GAIA facilitates progress in developing more autonomous and capable AI assistants.
