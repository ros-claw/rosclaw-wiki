> Standard span, metric, event, and attribute conventions for instrumenting LLM and agent workflows so harness traces stay portable across observability backends.

This project defines standard OpenTelemetry semantic conventions for tracing and monitoring generative AI systems, including LLMs and AI agents. It ensures that telemetry data like spans, metrics, events, and attributes are consistent across different observability backends. By standardizing instrumentation, it makes traces portable and interoperable between tools. The conventions support evals and observability of complex AI workflows.
