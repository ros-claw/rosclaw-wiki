Pay per call via Stripe. [shopgraph.dev](https://shopgraph.dev)

Shopgraph is an MCP server that extracts structured product data from the open web by combining Schema.org markup with AI-powered extraction. It enriches e-commerce listings with detailed product information, such as prices, descriptions, and specifications. The service operates on a pay-per-call model, with payments processed through Stripe. It is designed to help businesses automate product data enrichment for online stores.
