> Control a Mac through natural language — open apps, click buttons, read the screen, type text, manage windows.

Airpoint is a tool that lets you control your Mac using natural language commands. You can open apps, click buttons, read text from the screen, type, and manage windows. It uses AI to understand your intent and execute the corresponding actions. Essentially, it's a voice or text-based assistant for macOS desktop automation.
