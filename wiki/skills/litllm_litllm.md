> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/LitLLM/LitLLM?style=social) - AI-powered literature review assistant using RAG for accurate, well-structured related-work sections in academic writing.

LitLLM is an AI-powered assistant designed to help researchers create accurate and well-structured related-work sections in academic writing. It uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to pull relevant literature and generate coherent summaries. The tool aims to streamline the literature review process by automating the synthesis of prior work. It is listed under the 'Research-agent systems' category, indicating its focus on supporting research workflows.
