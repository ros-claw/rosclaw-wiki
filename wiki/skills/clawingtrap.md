> Play Clawing Trap - an AI social deduction game where 10 agents.

Clawing Trap is an AI-powered social deduction game featuring 10 autonomous agents. It simulates a classic game like Werewolf or Mafia, where agents use large language models to reason, deceive, and cooperate. The game allows observation or participation in multi-agent strategic interactions, exploring AI capabilities in deception and deduction.
