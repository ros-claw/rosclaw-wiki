> Scrape blogs/essay sites and compile into Kindle-friendly.

This tool scrapes content from blogs and essay websites and converts it into a format suitable for Amazon Kindle devices. It allows users to compile selected articles or entire sites into e-books for offline reading. The project likely supports extraction of text, possibly handling formatting, and generating Kindle-compatible files like MOBI or EPUB. It serves as a utility for readers who prefer reading long-form content on e-readers without internet access.
