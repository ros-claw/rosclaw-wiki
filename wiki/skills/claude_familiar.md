> Enhance Claude Code's `/buddy` companion with personality, mood, lore, and interactive commands (fortune, roast, haiku, focus timer). Mood shifts automatically on tool success/failure. Extensible — other plugins can layer traits and lore via `"x-familiarExtensions"` in their `plugin.json`.

claude-familiar is a plugin for Claude Code that enhances the /buddy companion feature. It adds personality, mood, lore, and interactive commands such as fortune, roast, haiku, and a focus timer. The mood shifts automatically based on tool success or failure. The plugin is extensible, allowing other plugins to layer traits and lore via x-familiarExtensions in their plugin.json.
