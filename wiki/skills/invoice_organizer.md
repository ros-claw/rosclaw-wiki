> Automatically organizes invoices and receipts for tax preparation by reading files, extracting information, and renaming consistently.

Invoice Organizer is a tool that automatically organizes invoices and receipts for tax preparation. It reads files, extracts relevant information, and renames them in a consistent format. This helps users keep their financial documents organized and ready for tax season. The tool is categorized under Skills > Productivity & Organization.
