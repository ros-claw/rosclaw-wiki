This project provides a set of skills for AI agents to perform materials simulation tasks, such as setting up and running molecular dynamics simulations, computing material properties, and analyzing results. The skills are modular and composable, allowing agents to construct complex workflows for materials science research. This enables embodied intelligence systems to autonomously conduct simulation-based experiments and accelerate discovery.
