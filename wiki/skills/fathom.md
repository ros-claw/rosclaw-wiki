> Connect to Fathom AI to fetch call recordings, transcripts, and summaries.

Fathom is a tool for connecting to Fathom AI to fetch call recordings, transcripts, and summaries. It provides an interface to retrieve meeting data from Fathom's AI-powered note-taking service. Users can integrate this tool into their workflows to access recorded calls and generated summaries. This is useful for automating meeting documentation and analysis.
