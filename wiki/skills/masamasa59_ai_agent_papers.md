> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/masamasa59/ai-agent-papers?style=social) - AI agent research papers updated biweekly via automated arxiv search with curated selection.

This project curates a biweekly-updated list of AI agent research papers, sourced automatically from arxiv search results and then manually selected for relevance. It serves as a central repository for keeping track of the latest developments in AI agent technology. The curated list helps researchers and practitioners stay informed about cutting-edge studies without needing to constantly scan arxiv themselves.
