> 🎀 AgenticMail — Full email, SMS, storage & multi-agent coordination for AI agents. 63 tools.

AgenticMail is a toolkit for AI agents that provides full email, SMS, storage, and multi-agent coordination capabilities, with a total of 63 tools. It enables agents to communicate through traditional channels like email and SMS, share data via storage, and coordinate their actions in multi-agent setups. This framework facilitates building complex agentic workflows that require persistent state and inter-agent messaging.
