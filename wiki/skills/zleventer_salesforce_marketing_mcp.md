This is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that integrates with Salesforce, providing 47 tools specifically designed for marketing and revenue operations teams. It includes tools for managing leads, contacts, accounts, campaigns, and campaign members, as well as tasks and 17 reporting tools. Reporting capabilities include campaign ROI, lead-source attribution, pipeline-by-campaign, multi-touch campaign influence, MQL trend, forecast summary, and integration with the native Salesforce Reports API. The server helps automate marketing workflows and analyze campaign performance directly through MCP-compatible interfaces.
