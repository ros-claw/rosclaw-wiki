> Tamper-resistant audit watchdog for Clawdbot agents.

Clauditor is a tool designed to provide tamper-resistant audit logging for Clawdbot agents. It acts as a watchdog to ensure that agent actions are recorded in a way that cannot be easily altered or deleted. This helps maintain accountability and traceability in autonomous agent systems. The tool is intended to enhance security and trust in AI agent operations.
