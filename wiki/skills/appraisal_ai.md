> Draft real estate appraisal reports with tracked changes.

Appraisal-ai is a tool that helps draft real estate appraisal reports using AI. It includes a tracked changes feature to review modifications made by the AI. This streamlines the process of creating professional appraisal documents. The tool is aimed at real estate professionals seeking efficient report generation.
