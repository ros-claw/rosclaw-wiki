> A benchmark for browser agents on common knowledge-work tasks, useful for comparing harnesses on realistic enterprise-style web workflows instead of toy browser tasks.

WorkArena is a benchmark designed to evaluate browser-based AI agents on common knowledge work tasks. It focuses on realistic enterprise-style web workflows instead of simple toy browser tasks. This allows for meaningful comparison of different agent harnesses on practical, complex scenarios. The benchmark aims to advance the development of AI agents that can assist with everyday office and business web applications.
