> Thoughtworks on constraining agents with concrete exemplars so they produce more consistent output.

This project explores a technique called 'anchoring' where AI agents are constrained by providing concrete exemplars or reference applications. By grounding the agent's behavior in specific examples, the output becomes more consistent and aligned with desired outcomes. It falls under the category of guardrails and safe autonomy, emphasizing practical methods to enforce constraints without rigid rules. The approach is inspired by Thoughtworks' work on making AI behavior more predictable and trustworthy.
