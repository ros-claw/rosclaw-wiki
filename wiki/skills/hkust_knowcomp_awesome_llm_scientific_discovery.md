> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/HKUST-KnowComp/Awesome-LLM-Scientific-Discovery?style=social) - EMNLP 2025 survey on LLMs in scientific discovery.

This project is an awesome list and survey paper (accepted at EMNLP 2025) that collects and categorizes research on using large language models (LLMs) for scientific discovery. It covers various applications of LLMs across scientific disciplines, including hypothesis generation, experiment design, data analysis, and literature review. The resource is maintained by HKUST-KnowComp and aims to serve as a comprehensive reference for researchers in this growing interdisciplinary field.
