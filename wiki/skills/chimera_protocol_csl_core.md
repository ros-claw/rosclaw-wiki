> 🐍 🏠 🍎 🪟 🐧 - Deterministic AI safety policy engine with Z3 formal verification. Write, verify, and enforce machine-verifiable constraints for AI agents via MCP.

Chimera-Protocol/csl-core is a deterministic AI safety policy engine that leverages Z3 for formal verification. It enables users to write, verify, and enforce machine-verifiable constraints for AI agents. These constraints are applied via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), ensuring that AI behaviors adhere to predefined safety rules. The tool focuses on rigorous, provable safety guarantees for AI systems.
