Spectrawl is a unified web layer for AI agents that provides search capabilities across 8 engines, stealth browsing with cookie authentication, and the ability to act on 24 platforms. It offers 5,000 free searches per month via Google's Gemini Grounded Search. The tool is designed to simplify data extraction and interaction with web services for AI agents.
