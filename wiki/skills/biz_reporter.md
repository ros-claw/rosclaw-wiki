> Automated business intelligence reports pulling data from Google Analytics GA4, Google Search Console, Stripe.

Biz-reporter is a tool that automates the generation of business intelligence reports by pulling data from Google Analytics GA4, Google Search Console, and Stripe. It helps users consolidate data from multiple sources into a single report, saving time on manual data collection and analysis. The reports are suitable for monitoring key metrics like website traffic, search performance, and revenue trends.
