> Loads LLM configuration files to context, importing specific terminology, model configurations, and establishing baseline terminology for AI discussions.

This slash command loads LLM configuration files into context, importing specific terminology and model configurations. It establishes a shared baseline vocabulary for AI discussions, ensuring consistent understanding across conversations. The tool helps users prime their AI interactions with predefined settings and terms.
