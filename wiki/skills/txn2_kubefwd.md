> 🏎️ 🏠 - Kubernetes bulk port forwarding with service discovery, /etc/hosts management, traffic monitoring, and pod log streaming

Kubefwd is a command-line tool for Kubernetes that simplifies port forwarding by allowing bulk forwarding of multiple services at once. It automatically manages /etc/hosts entries for service discovery and provides traffic monitoring and pod log streaming features. This tool enhances developer productivity by eliminating the need for repetitive manual 'kubectl port-forward' commands.
