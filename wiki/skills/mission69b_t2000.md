mission69b/t2000 is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that provides non-custodial decentralized finance (DeFi) banking services for AI agents on the Sui blockchain. It includes 35 tools and 20 prompts enabling AI agents to save, borrow, invest, exchange, send, and pay assets autonomously. The system automatically optimizes yields across multiple Sui DeFi platforms, including NAVI, Suilend, and Cetus. This allows AI agents to manage financial operations without manual intervention while maintaining full custody of their assets.
