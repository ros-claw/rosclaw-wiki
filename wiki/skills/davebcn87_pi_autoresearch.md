> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/davebcn87/pi-autoresearch?style=social) - `pi` extension plus dashboard for persistent experiment loops, live metrics, confidence tracking, and resumable autoresearch sessions.

pi-autoresearch is an extension for the pi system that adds a dashboard for running persistent, automated research experiments. It tracks live metrics and confidence levels, and allows users to resume sessions. The tool is designed for continuous, autonomous experiment loops, making it easier to manage long-running AI research tasks.
