Auto-Research is an open-source AI agent designed to automate the entire research workflow, from query formulation to report generation. It leverages large language models to conduct web searches, extract and synthesize information, and produce comprehensive summaries or papers. The system aims to reduce manual research effort by intelligently navigating sources and organizing findings. This project is listed under research-agent systems, indicating its focus on autonomous information gathering and analysis.
