> A benchmark for evaluating agents on historical prediction market data, testing harness design for research, retrieval, and forecasting in long-horizon reasoning tasks.

The Olas Predict Benchmark evaluates AI agents on historical prediction market data, focusing on long-horizon reasoning tasks. It tests retrieval and forecasting capabilities through a structured harness. The benchmark provides a realistic environment for assessing agent performance over extended timeframes.
