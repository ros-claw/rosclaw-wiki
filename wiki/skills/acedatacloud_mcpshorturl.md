> [![AceDataCloud/MCPShortURL MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/AceDataCloud/MCPShortURL/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/AceDataCloud/MCPShortURL) 🐍 ☁️ - Free URL shortening with batch support (up to 10 URLs), permanent `surl.id` short links, zero credit consumption.

AceDataCloud/MCPShortURL is a free URL shortener server that works with the Model Context Protocol. It can shorten up to 10 URLs at once and creates permanent short links on the surl.id domain. The service does not use any credits, making it cost-free to use. It is written in Python and listed under other tools and integrations for MCP servers.
