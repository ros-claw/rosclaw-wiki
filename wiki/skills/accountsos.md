> AI-native accounting for UK micro-businesses.

AccountsOS is an AI-native accounting platform designed for UK micro-businesses. It uses artificial intelligence to automate financial tasks like bookkeeping and tax calculations. The platform aims to simplify accounting for very small businesses in the UK. It focuses on compliance and efficiency.
