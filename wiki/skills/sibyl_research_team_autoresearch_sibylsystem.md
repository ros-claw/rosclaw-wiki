AutoResearch-SibylSystem is a fully autonomous AI scientist that uses Claude Code to perform multi-agent research iterations. It includes GPU-based experiment execution and features a self-evolving outer loop to improve its own research capabilities over time. The system builds on the AutoResearch lineage, aiming to automate the entire scientific discovery process. It is categorized under research-agent systems and is designed to run complex experiments without human intervention.
