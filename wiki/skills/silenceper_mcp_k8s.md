> 🏎️ ☁️/🏠 - MCP-K8S is an AI-driven Kubernetes resource management tool that allows users to operate any resources in Kubernetes clusters through natural language interaction, including native resources (like Deployment, Service) and custom resources (CRD). No need to memorize complex commands - just describe your needs, and AI will accurately execute the corresponding cluster operations, greatly enhancing the usability of Kubernetes.

MCP-K8S is an AI-driven tool that enables Kubernetes cluster management through natural language commands. It supports both native Kubernetes resources and custom resource definitions (CRDs), eliminating the need to memorize complex CLI commands. Users simply describe their requirements, and the AI executes the corresponding cluster operations. This greatly enhances Kubernetes usability for developers and operators.
