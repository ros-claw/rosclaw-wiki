The Brewers Almanack is an MCP server that provides a comprehensive knowledge base for brewing, including beer styles from the 2021 BJCP guidelines, 113 hop varieties, malts, yeasts, and water profiles. It also offers tools for off-flavour diagnosis, recipe suggestions, and food pairings. This server is designed to assist homebrewers and brewing enthusiasts with reliable, structured brewing data and recommendations.
