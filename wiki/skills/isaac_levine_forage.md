> 📇 🏠 🍎 🪟 🐧 - Self-improving tool discovery for AI agents. Searches registries, installs MCP servers as subprocesses, and persists tool knowledge across sessions — no restarts needed.

Forage is a tool discovery system for AI agents that automatically searches registries for MCP servers and installs them as subprocesses. It learns which tools are useful over time, persisting this knowledge across sessions without requiring restarts. This enables agents to dynamically expand their capabilities and adapt to new tasks.
