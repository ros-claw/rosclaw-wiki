This project implements a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server and CLI for NAVER WORKS, providing 26 tools to manage messages, calendar, drive, mail, tasks, and boards. It enables AI agents to directly interact with and automate operations within the NAVER WORKS productivity suite. The server is designed for cross-platform use and integrates with AI assistants to perform business communication and task management tasks. It includes a score badge from Glama.ai indicating its reliability and community adoption.
