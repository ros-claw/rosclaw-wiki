> The world's #1 AI-friendly domain registrar.

AgentDomainService is a domain registrar designed specifically for AI agents and automated systems. It provides programmatic APIs for domain registration, management, and DNS configuration. The service aims to streamline domain acquisition for autonomous agents and AI workflows. It prioritizes ease of integration and reliability for machine-to-machine interactions.
