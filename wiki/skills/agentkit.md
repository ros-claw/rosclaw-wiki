> Inngest's TypeScript toolkit for building durable, workflow-aware agents on top of event-driven infrastructure.

AgentKit is a TypeScript toolkit developed by Inngest for creating durable and workflow-aware agents. It leverages event-driven infrastructure to manage agent state and execution, enabling complex workflows with resilience. The toolkit is categorized under runtimes, harnesses, and reference implementations in the agent ecosystem. It is designed to integrate with Inngest's event-driven platform, making it suitable for building production-grade agents.
