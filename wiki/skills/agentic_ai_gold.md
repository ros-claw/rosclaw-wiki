> The only agent framework that improves itself while you sleep.

Agentic AI Gold is a framework for building AI agents that autonomously improve their performance over time, even while the developer is not actively working. It learns from past interactions and refines its behavior without manual intervention. The framework aims to reduce the need for constant tuning by enabling self-optimization. This allows agents to become more efficient and effective with continued use.
