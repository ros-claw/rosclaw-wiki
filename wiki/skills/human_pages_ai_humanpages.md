HumanPages is an MCP server that provides a marketplace for real human workers to be discovered and hired by AI agents. Agents can search for workers based on skills, location, and equipment, send job offers, post listings, and message workers directly. The platform offers a free tier, a Pro subscription, and a pay-per-use model using x402 micropayments. It is designed to integrate with MCP-compatible AI systems to enable autonomous hiring of human labor.
