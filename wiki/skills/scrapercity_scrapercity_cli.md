Scrapercity CLI is an MCP server that provides over 20 tools for B2B lead generation, including Apollo, Google Maps, email finder, email validator, mobile finder, skip trace, and ecommerce store data extraction. It is designed to automate data extraction and lead enrichment tasks, making it suitable for integration with AI agents and assistants that support the Model Context Protocol. The tool helps users efficiently gather and validate business contact information for sales and marketing purposes.
