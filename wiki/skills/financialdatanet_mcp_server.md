> 🐍 🏠 🪟 🐧 - [FinancialData.Net](https://financialdata.net/) MCP server allows you to retrieve end-of-day and intraday stock market data, company financial statements, insider and institutional trading data, sustainability data, earnings releases, and much more.

This MCP server integrates with FinancialData.net to provide access to a wide range of financial and stock market data. It supports retrieval of end-of-day and intraday prices, company financial statements, insider and institutional trading activity, sustainability metrics, and earnings releases. Designed for developers and financial analysts, it enables automated data fetching within the MCP ecosystem.
