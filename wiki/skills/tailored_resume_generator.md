> Analyzes job descriptions and generates tailored resumes that highlight relevant experience, skills, and achievements to maximize interview chances.

This tool analyzes job descriptions to automatically generate customized resumes. It highlights the user's most relevant experience, skills, and achievements for each application. The goal is to increase the chance of getting an interview by tailoring the resume to specific job requirements.
