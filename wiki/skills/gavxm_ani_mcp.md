> [![ani-mcp MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/gavxm/ani-mcp/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/gavxm/ani-mcp) 📇 🏠 - MCP server for AniList with taste-aware recommendations, watch analytics, social tools, and full list management.

ani-mcp is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that connects AI assistants to the AniList anime and manga tracking platform. It enables users to get personalized recommendations based on their taste, analyze their watching habits, manage their anime/manga lists, and access social features. The server exposes tools for querying and updating AniList data, making it useful for anime enthusiasts who want AI-assisted list management and discovery.
