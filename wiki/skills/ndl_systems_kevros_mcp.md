Free tier: 100 calls/month.

Kevros-MCP is a security-focused server that provides governance primitives for autonomous AI agents. It allows agents to verify their actions against predefined policies, record signed provenance of those actions, and cryptographically bind intents. The server includes a free tier offering 100 calls per month and is designed to integrate with the Model Context Protocol ecosystem.
