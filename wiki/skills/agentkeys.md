> Secure credential proxy for AI agents.

Agentkeys is a tool that provides a secure way to handle credentials for AI agents. It acts as a proxy, allowing agents to access necessary secrets without exposing them directly. This helps maintain security while enabling AI agents to perform tasks that require authentication. The project focuses on credential management in AI applications.
