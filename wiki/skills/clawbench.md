> A benchmark that evaluates AI agents across search, reasoning, coding, safety, and multi-turn conversation tasks, covering the breadth of harness demands in a single suite.

ClawBench is a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents across a wide range of tasks including search, reasoning, coding, safety, and multi-turn conversation. It aims to cover the full spectrum of harness demands in a single suite, making it a versatile tool for assessing agent capabilities. By integrating these dimensions, ClawBench provides a holistic evaluation framework for AI agents.
