> Punk-style ADHD body doubling for founders.

ADHD body doubling is a productivity technique where a person works alongside another to improve focus and accountability. This project gives it a punk-style aesthetic, likely appealing to nonconformist or creative individuals. It is specifically designed for startup founders who struggle with ADHD and need structured co-working. The punk twist adds a rebellious, energetic vibe to the traditional method.
