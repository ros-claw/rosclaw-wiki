> Generates creative domain name ideas and checks availability across multiple TLDs including .com, .io, .dev, and .ai extensions.

This tool generates creative domain name ideas for websites and businesses. It checks the availability of these names across multiple top-level domains (TLDs) including .com, .io, .dev, and .ai. The brainstorming process helps users come up with unique and memorable names. It is useful for entrepreneurs and marketers looking to establish an online presence.
