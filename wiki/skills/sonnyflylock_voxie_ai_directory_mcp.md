> 📇 ☁️ - AI Phone Number Directory providing access to AI services via webchat. Query Voxie AI personas and third-party services like ChatGPT, with instant webchat URLs for free interactions.

This MCP server acts as an aggregator directory for AI services, providing phone numbers and instant webchat URLs to interact with AI personas. It supports querying Voxie AI personas and third-party services like ChatGPT, enabling free webchat interactions. The tool centralizes access to multiple AI agents through a simple phone-number-based lookup.
