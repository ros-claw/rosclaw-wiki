The AI-Scientist project by SakanaAI is an open-source research-agent system that automates the entire scientific discovery pipeline. It leverages large language models and other AI tools to generate hypotheses, design experiments, and analyze results without human intervention. The system aims to accelerate research by acting as an autonomous scientist, capable of iterating on ideas and producing novel contributions. This project represents a significant step towards fully automated scientific discovery.
