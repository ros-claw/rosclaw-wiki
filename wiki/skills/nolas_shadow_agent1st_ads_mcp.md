This MCP server enables AI agents to manage Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok ad campaigns with a single tool call, handling targeting, creative, budget, and ad creation. It is listed under Marketing server implementations and requires a license from agent1st.io/ads. The service simplifies campaign management for automated advertising workflows.
