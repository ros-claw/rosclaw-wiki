> Proactive monitoring of Aave V3 borrow positions with liquidation alerts.

This tool monitors Aave V3 borrowing positions to detect and alert users about potential liquidations. It provides proactive notifications when loan-to-value thresholds approach risky levels. By tracking positions in real-time, it helps users take action before assets are forcibly liquidated. The system is designed for DeFi participants managing leveraged positions on Aave.
