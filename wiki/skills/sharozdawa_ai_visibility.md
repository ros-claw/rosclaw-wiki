This MCP server tracks brand visibility across major AI chatbots including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. It provides visibility scores, sentiment analysis, competitor detection, and trend charts. Users can monitor how often their brand is mentioned or discussed in AI-generated responses. The tool is designed for marketing teams to gauge brand awareness in the AI ecosystem.
