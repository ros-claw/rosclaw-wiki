This MCP server integrates Suunto watches with AI tools via the official apizone API. It supports OAuth2 authentication, allowing users to list and retrieve workouts, decode FIT files, and export GPX data. It also provides 24/7 health metrics such as activity, sleep, and recovery/HRV data. Resources expose today's recovery status and weekly training summary as ambient context for AI agents.
