> Encrypted backups, integrity verification, and data retention enforcement for Greek legal requirements (5-20 year.

This project provides tools for creating encrypted backups with integrity verification. It ensures data retention periods as required by Greek law, typically 5 to 20 years. The system automates backup processes while maintaining data integrity through cryptographic checksums. It enforces retention policies to comply with legal requirements.
