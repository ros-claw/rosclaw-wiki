> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/peterskoett/self-improving-agent?style=social) - Alternative self-improving agent architecture with reflection and meta-learning cycles.

This project proposes an alternative architecture for self-improving AI agents that leverages reflection and meta-learning cycles. It enables the agent to continuously learn from its own actions and optimize its behavior over time. The design is positioned as a general-purpose descendant within the ecosystem of autonomous agents, aiming to enhance performance and adaptability.
