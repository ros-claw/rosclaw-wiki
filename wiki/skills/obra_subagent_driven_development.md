Subagent-driven development is a methodology for software development where the process is broken down into smaller, autonomous sub-agents that handle specific tasks like coding, testing, and debugging. These sub-agents can collaborate and communicate to achieve larger development goals, often under human oversight. The approach aims to increase efficiency and parallelization by distributing work among multiple AI-powered agents.
