> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gersteinlab/ML-Bench?style=social) - Evaluates LLMs and agents for ML tasks on repository-level code.

ML-Bench is a benchmark for evaluating large language models and AI agents on machine learning tasks that involve repository-level code. It focuses on assessing the ability to understand and manipulate entire codebases, simulating realistic ML development scenarios. The benchmark helps compare performance across different models and agents, advancing AI-assisted coding and ML automation.
