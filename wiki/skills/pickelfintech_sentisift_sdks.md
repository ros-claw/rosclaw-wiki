Sentisift SDKs provide comment moderation and intelligence for articles, including bot and spam detection, multilingual sentiment analysis, and constructive comment generation via its Influence feature on paid tiers. It offers a free tier allowing up to 1,000 comments without requiring a credit card. The service is designed for marketing and content management applications, helping publishers maintain quality discussions.
