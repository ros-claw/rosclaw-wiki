> Use cheap, TEE-verified AI models from the 0G Compute Network as OpenClaw providers.

This project enables users to access low-cost AI models that are verified through Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) from the 0G Compute Network, and then use them as providers within the OpenClaw framework. It aims to reduce costs while maintaining security and verifiability of AI computations. The integration allows decentralized applications to leverage verified AI models without relying on centralized providers.
