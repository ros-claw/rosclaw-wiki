> When the user wants to manage, automate, or analyze paid advertising campaigns on Google Ads, Meta.

This agent automates and analyzes paid advertising campaigns on Google Ads and Meta platforms, helping users manage their ad spend efficiently. It integrates with both ad networks to support campaign creation, optimization, and performance tracking. The goal is to reduce manual effort in adjusting bids, targeting, and budgets while providing actionable insights. It serves as a tool for marketers to streamline their advertising operations through conversational or scripted interfaces.
