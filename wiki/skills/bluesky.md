> Complete Bluesky CLI: post, reply, like, repost, follow, block, mute, search,.

bluesky is a command-line interface tool that provides complete functionality for the Bluesky social network. It enables users to perform actions like posting, replying, liking, reposting, following, blocking, muting, and searching directly from the terminal. The tool is built on the AT Protocol, allowing efficient interaction with the decentralized social platform. It is useful for developers and power users who prefer a text-based workflow.
