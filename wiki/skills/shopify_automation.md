> Automate Shopify: products, orders, customers, inventory, and GraphQL queries.

Shopify Automation is a tool that integrates with Composio to automate various Shopify operations. It can handle tasks related to products, orders, customers, inventory, and execute GraphQL queries. This enables seamless app automation for e-commerce workflows without manual intervention.
