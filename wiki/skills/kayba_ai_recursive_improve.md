> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/kayba-ai/recursive-improve?style=social) - Recursive self-improvement framework where agents capture execution traces, analyze failure patterns, and apply targeted fixes with keep-or-revert evaluation.

This framework enables recursive self-improvement for AI agents by capturing execution traces, analyzing failure patterns, and applying targeted fixes. It includes a keep-or-revert evaluation to ensure changes are beneficial. The approach allows agents to autonomously enhance their performance over time through iterative refinement.
