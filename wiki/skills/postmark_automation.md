> Automate Postmark: transactional emails, templates, servers, and delivery stats.

Postmark Automation is a tool that leverages Composio to automate various Postmark email services, including sending transactional emails, managing templates, configuring servers, and tracking delivery statistics. It enables users to integrate these tasks into automated workflows, reducing manual effort. The automation interacts with Postmark's API to perform operations like sending emails, updating server settings, and retrieving analytics. This tool is part of a larger suite of app automations provided by Composio, aimed at streamlining email operations.
