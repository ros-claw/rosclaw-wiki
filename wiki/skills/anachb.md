> Austrian public transport (VOR AnachB) for all of Austria.

Anachb is a project providing access to Austrian public transport data, specifically the VOR AnachB system. It covers all of Austria, integrating various regional transport associations. The tool likely allows users to query schedules, routes, and other transit information. It serves as a digital interface for the VOR (Verkehrsverbund Ost-Region) and potentially other Austrian transit networks.
