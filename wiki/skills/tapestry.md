> Interlink and summarize related documents into knowledge networks.

Tapestry is a tool that helps users interlink and summarize related documents, turning them into knowledge networks. It enables efficient organization of information by creating connections between concepts and generating concise overviews. This approach aids in discovering relationships and patterns across a collection of documents, enhancing understanding and recall.
