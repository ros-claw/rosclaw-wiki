> Define and store your brand voice profile for consistent content generation.

Brand-voice-profile is a tool for defining and storing a brand's unique voice and tone. It enables consistent content generation by providing a reusable profile that can be referenced by AI agents or content generators. This helps maintain a cohesive brand identity across various outputs, from marketing copy to customer communications. The profile typically includes attributes like formality, vocabulary, and sentiment preferences.
