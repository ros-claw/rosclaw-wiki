> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/JinheonBaek/ResearchAgent?style=social) - Iterative research idea generation over scientific literature with LLMs. Multi-agent review and feedback loops.

ResearchAgent is a system that leverages large language models to iteratively generate novel research ideas by analyzing scientific literature. It employs a multi-agent architecture where agents review and provide feedback in a loop, refining ideas over successive iterations. The goal is to assist researchers in discovering promising research directions grounded in existing knowledge.
