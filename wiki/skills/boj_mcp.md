> Access Bank of Japan (BOJ/日本銀行) statistical data — price indices (CGPI, SPPI), flow of funds, balance of payments.

This project provides a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server to access Bank of Japan (BOJ) statistical data. It allows querying key economic indicators such as Corporate Goods Price Index (CGPI), Services Producer Price Index (SPPI), flow of funds, and balance of payments. Users can integrate this tool with AI agents to fetch real-time or historical BOJ data. The tool simplifies access to Japanese macroeconomic statistics for analysis and automation.
