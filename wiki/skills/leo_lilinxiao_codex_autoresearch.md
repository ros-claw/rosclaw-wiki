> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/leo-lilinxiao/codex-autoresearch?style=social) - Codex-native autoresearch skill with resume support, lessons across runs, optional parallel experiments, and mode-specific workflows.

Codex-autoresearch is a tool that leverages OpenAI's Codex model to autonomously conduct research experiments. It supports resuming interrupted runs, accumulating lessons learned across multiple runs, and running parallel experiments for efficiency. The tool also adapts to mode-specific workflows, making it flexible for various research tasks. This allows researchers to automate complex experimental loops with Codex as the core reasoning engine.
