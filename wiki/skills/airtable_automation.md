> Automate Airtable: records, tables, bases, views, and field management.

This project enables automation of Airtable operations through the Composio platform. It covers managing records, creating and modifying tables, handling multiple bases, working with views, and configuring fields. Users can integrate these capabilities into larger automation workflows without manual interaction. The tool simplifies repetitive data management tasks within Airtable.
