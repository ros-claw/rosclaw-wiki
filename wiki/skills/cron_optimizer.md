> Optimizes system cron jobs by removing stale, disabled, or redundant entries to reduce exec noise.

The cron-optimizer scans system cron job configurations to identify and remove entries that are stale, disabled, or redundant. By cleaning up unnecessary cron jobs, it reduces the number of pointless executions, thereby lowering system noise. This helps improve system performance and makes cron schedules easier to maintain. The tool is designed for system administrators who want to keep their cron configurations tidy and efficient.
