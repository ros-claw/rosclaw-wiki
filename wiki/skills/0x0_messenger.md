> Send and receive P2P messages using disposable numbers and PINs.

0x0-messenger enables peer-to-peer messaging without account registration by using disposable phone numbers and PINs for temporary identity. This design enhances privacy and security by limiting exposure of personal data. Messages are encrypted and delivered directly between users, providing a simple and ephemeral communication method.
