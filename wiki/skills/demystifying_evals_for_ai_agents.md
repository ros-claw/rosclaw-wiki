> Anthropic's guidance on what to measure when agents have many possible trajectories to success or failure.

Demystifying Evals for AI Agents provides guidance from Anthropic on how to evaluate AI agents that have many possible trajectories to success or failure. It focuses on what metrics and observations are important for these complex systems. The project falls under the category of Evals and Observability, aiming to help practitioners measure agent behavior effectively. By understanding the unique challenges of agent evaluation, developers can build more reliable and safe AI systems.
