> Manage DAKboard screens, devices, and push custom display data.

dakboard is a tool for managing DAKboard screens and devices. It allows users to push custom display data to DAKboard displays, enabling automated content updates. This simplifies the configuration and control of DAKboard screens for various use cases like home dashboards or digital signage.
