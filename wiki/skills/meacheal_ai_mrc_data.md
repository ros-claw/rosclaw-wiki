> 📇 ☁️ - China's apparel supply chain data for AI agents. 1,000+ verified suppliers, 350+ lab-tested fabrics, 170+ industrial clusters with AATCC / ISO / GB lab-test verification.

This project offers a structured dataset of China's apparel supply chain, including over 1,000 verified suppliers, 350+ lab-tested fabrics, and 170+ industrial clusters. The data is verified using industry standards such as AATCC, ISO, and GB. It is specifically designed for AI agents to access and use for tasks like supplier sourcing, quality assurance, and supply chain analysis. The goal is to improve transparency and efficiency in the apparel industry.
