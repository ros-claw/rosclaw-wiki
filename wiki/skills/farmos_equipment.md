> Query equipment status, maintenance schedules, and service history for the farm fleet.

This tool allows users to query equipment status, maintenance schedules, and service history for farm fleet machinery. It is designed to help manage agricultural equipment efficiently by providing easy access to operational data. The project integrates with FarmOS to enable fleet tracking and maintenance planning. Users can monitor equipment availability, schedule repairs, and review past service records.
