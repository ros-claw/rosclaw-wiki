> Multi-channel ABM automation that turns LinkedIn URLs.

abm-outbound is a tool for automating multi-channel account-based marketing campaigns by processing LinkedIn URLs. It ingests LinkedIn profile or company URLs and uses them to initiate outbound sequences across channels like email and social media. The automation reduces manual effort in targeting specific accounts and integrates with CRM systems to manage outreach pipelines efficiently.
