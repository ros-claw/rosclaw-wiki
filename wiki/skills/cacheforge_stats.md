> CacheForge terminal dashboard — usage, savings, and performance metrics.

CacheForge Stats is a terminal dashboard that displays usage, savings, and performance metrics for CacheForge. It provides real-time monitoring of caching operations directly from the command line. Users can track cache hit rates, bandwidth savings, and other key performance indicators. The tool is designed for developers and system administrators who need quick visibility into their caching infrastructure.
