> Render music videos from audio files and lyrics using Remotion.

This project provides a tool to render music videos from audio files and lyrics. It uses the Remotion framework to generate video content that syncs lyrics with the audio. Users can input an audio track and corresponding lyrics, and the tool outputs a music video with visual elements. It is designed for simplicity, making it accessible for creators to produce music videos programmatically.
