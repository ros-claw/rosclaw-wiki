> 🐍 ☁️ - Regulatory compliance (AI Act, GDPR, DORA) with article-level citations

Buildsyncinc/gibs-mcp is a server implementation focused on legal and regulatory compliance, specifically addressing the AI Act, GDPR, and DORA. It provides article-level citations for these regulations, helping developers ensure their systems adhere to European Union legal standards. The tool integrates with MCP to offer compliance checking in AI agent workflows. It is listed under Server Implementations for legal purposes, indicating a specialized use case.
