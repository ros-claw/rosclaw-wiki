> OpenAI's general guide to building eval suites that match real-world distributions and catch regressions early.

This guide from OpenAI outlines best practices for building evaluation suites for AI models. It emphasizes the importance of matching the evaluation data to real-world distributions to ensure the model performs well in actual use. The guide also covers techniques for catching regressions early in the development cycle, such as continuous evaluation and automated testing. It provides a structured approach to designing, maintaining, and iterating on eval suites that are both comprehensive and practical. Overall, it aims to help teams improve model reliability and trustworthiness through systematic evaluation.
