> Your personal knowledge repository — capture, organize, and retrieve.

Brainrepo is a personal knowledge repository that helps users capture, organize, and retrieve information efficiently. It acts as a second brain for managing notes, ideas, and references. Users can structure their knowledge using tags, links, and categories, enabling easy search and recall. The tool emphasizes local storage and plain-text formats for privacy and portability.
