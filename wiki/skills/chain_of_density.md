> Iteratively densify text summaries using Chain-of-Density technique.

Chain-of-Density is a technique for iteratively densifying text summaries, meaning it adds more informative content without increasing length unnecessarily. It works by repeatedly refining a summary to pack in more key details while maintaining coherence. This method is useful for generating concise yet information-rich summaries from longer texts. It falls under the broader field of natural language processing and text summarization.
