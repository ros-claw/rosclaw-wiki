Specialized tuning agents deliver sovereign models trained on your data. Supports Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral, Gemma 1B-72B. LoRA, QLoRA, full fine-tuning. Cost estimation, model management, S3 export.

A command-line tool that enables fine-tuning of open-source large and small language models without requiring any infrastructure management. It uses specialized tuning agents to create custom, privately owned models trained on user-provided data, supporting popular architectures like Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Gemma. The tool offers various fine-tuning methods including LoRA, QLoRA, and full fine-tuning, along with features for cost estimation, model management, and exporting models to S3 storage. Designed to give users sovereign control over their fine-tuned models.
