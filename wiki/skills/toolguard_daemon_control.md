> Manage long-running processes as macOS launchd services.

Toolguard-daemon-control is a tool for managing long-running processes as macOS launchd services. It integrates with launchd to start, stop, and monitor daemon processes, providing a convenient interface for system administrators. The tool likely simplifies the configuration and control of persistent background services on macOS.
