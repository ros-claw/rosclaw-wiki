> Anthropic on reducing approval friction without losing control through better sandboxing and policy design.

Anthropic proposes moving beyond frequent permission prompts to increase Claude Code's autonomy while maintaining security. They advocate for better sandboxing and policy design as alternatives to friction-heavy approval gates. This approach aims to reduce human intervention without compromising control. The work falls under the category of constraints, guardrails, and safe autonomy.
