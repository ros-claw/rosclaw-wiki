> Thoughtworks on moving quality checks into the loop instead of relying on after-the-fact manual review.

This project focuses on integrating quality checks directly into the coding workflow when using an AI agent, rather than relying on post-development manual reviews. Thoughtworks advocates for real-time quality assessment to catch issues earlier and ensure higher internal quality from the outset. The approach aims to make quality assurance an intrinsic, continuous part of agent-assisted coding, reducing the need for after-the-fact corrections.
