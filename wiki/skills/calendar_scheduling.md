> Schedule and book across Google, Outlook, and CalDAV.

calendar-scheduling is a tool for scheduling and booking events across multiple calendar platforms including Google Calendar, Outlook, and CalDAV-compatible services. It allows users to manage appointments, meetings, and bookings from a unified interface. The project aims to simplify cross-platform calendar integration for personal and professional use.
