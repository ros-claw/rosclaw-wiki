> Track data origin, transformations.

Data-lineage-tracker is a tool designed to monitor and record the origin and transformation of data across pipelines. It helps organizations maintain data provenance, ensuring transparency and compliance with governance policies. By tracking how data moves and changes, it aids in debugging, auditing, and impact analysis. The tool typically integrates with ETL workflows and metadata stores to provide end-to-end visibility.
