> 📇 ☁️ 🍎 🪟 🐧 - B2B sales intelligence and prospecting with Apollo.io. Search for prospects, enrich contacts with emails and phone numbers, discover companies by industry and size, and access Apollo's database of 275M+ contacts.

This project provides a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that interfaces with Apollo.io's API for B2B sales intelligence and prospecting. It allows users to search for prospects, enrich contact data with emails and phone numbers, discover companies filtered by industry and size, and access a database of over 275 million contacts. The tool is designed for sales and marketing teams to streamline lead generation and outreach.
