Free tier, no card. Install: `npx -y @deliveriq/mcp`.

This MCP server provides AI agents with comprehensive email deliverability analysis tools, including single and batch verification, DNS blocklist checks across 50 zones, and infrastructure analysis of SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, and BIMI records. It also features spam-trap scoring based on 13 weighted signals and a composite domain trust report. The server operates with a 5-stage, 21-check pipeline and offers a free tier without requiring a credit card.
