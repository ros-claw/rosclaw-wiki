gorilla-mcp is a hosted MCP server that helps solo SaaS founders discover leads by searching Reddit, X, YouTube, and TikTok for posts describing problems their product solves. It ranks each lead by buying intent and provides tools like `find_leads`, `draft_outreach`, and `plan_acquisition_funnel` to manage the full customer acquisition flow. The service costs $0.99 per run and can be installed via npx from GitHub. It is listed under Marketing implementations and requires signing up at usegorilla.app.
