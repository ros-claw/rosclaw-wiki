> Generate professional advertising images from product URLs.

Ad-ready-pro is a tool that takes a product URL as input and automatically generates professional-quality advertising images for that product. It likely uses web scraping to extract product images and metadata, then applies image processing or AI-based generation to create ad visuals. The tool aims to streamline the ad creation process for e-commerce or marketing teams, saving time and effort in producing consistent promotional material.
