> Start every day focused.

This project creates an AI-generated daily briefing to help users start their day with focus. It aggregates and summarizes personalized information from multiple sources, such as news and tasks, to reduce cognitive load. The tool automates the curation of essential updates, allowing users to quickly catch up on what matters most.
