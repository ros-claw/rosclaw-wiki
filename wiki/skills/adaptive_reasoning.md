> Automatically assess task complexity and adjust reasoning level.

This project automatically assesses the complexity of a given task and adjusts the level of reasoning applied accordingly. It aims to optimize computational resource usage by avoiding overthinking simple tasks while ensuring sufficient depth for complex ones. The approach likely involves dynamic allocation of reasoning depth or model size based on predicted difficulty.
