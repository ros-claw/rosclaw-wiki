> 📇 ☁️ - Korean & global crypto exchange routing, arbitrage signals, and x402 USDC micropayments for AI agents. 16 tools, 9 exchanges, 11 bridge coins.

Crossfin is a server implementation that provides tools for Korean and global cryptocurrency exchange routing, arbitrage signal detection, and USDC micropayments for AI agents. It supports 16 tools across 9 exchanges and 11 bridge coins, enabling efficient cross-exchange transactions. The system is designed to facilitate automated arbitrage strategies and seamless micropayment workflows for AI-driven agents.
