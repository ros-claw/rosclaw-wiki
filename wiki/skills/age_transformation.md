> Transform faces across ages using each::sense AI.

The age-transformation project leverages each::sense AI to alter facial images, making them appear older or younger. It uses machine learning models to preserve the individual's identity while realistically modifying age-related characteristics. This technology has applications in entertainment, research, and forensic science. The system relies on advanced deep learning techniques, such as generative adversarial networks, to produce convincing age-progressed or age-regressed faces.
