> ezBookkeeping is a lightweight, self-hosted personal finance app.

ezBookkeeping is a lightweight, self-hosted personal finance app that allows users to manage their finances privately and securely. It is designed to be easy to deploy and use, offering core bookkeeping features without relying on external cloud services. The application is open-source, giving users full control over their financial data. It is suitable for individuals or small businesses seeking a simple, private alternative to cloud-based finance tools.
