> [![dsers-mcp-product MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/lofder/dsers-mcp-product/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/lofder/dsers-mcp-product) 📇 ☁️ - Automate AliExpress/Alibaba dropshipping product import to Shopify or Wix via DSers. Bulk import, variant editing, pricing rules, and multi-store push with a single command.

This is an MCP server that integrates with DSers to automate the import of products from AliExpress and Alibaba into Shopify or Wix stores. It supports bulk importing, editing product variants, setting pricing rules, and pushing products to multiple stores with a single command. Designed for dropshipping workflows, it simplifies the product management process and enhances efficiency for e-commerce merchants.
