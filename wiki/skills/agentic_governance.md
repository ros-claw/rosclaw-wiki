> Keep your constraints healthy — lifecycle management with automatic staleness detection.

Agentic-governance is a tool for managing constraints in AI agent systems, focusing on lifecycle management. It automatically detects stale constraints to keep them healthy and up-to-date. This helps ensure that agent behaviors remain aligned with current requirements. The project is part of the embodied-intelligence and AI-agent tooling ecosystem.
