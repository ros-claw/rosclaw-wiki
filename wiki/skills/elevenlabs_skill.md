> Text-to-speech, sound effects, music generation, voice.

The elevenlabs-skill is an integration module for voice assistants that leverages ElevenLabs' API to provide text-to-speech, sound effects, and music generation. It enables natural, high-quality voice output and audio effects directly from a conversational AI platform. This skill can be used to add expressive speech, background sounds, or generative music to assistant interactions.
