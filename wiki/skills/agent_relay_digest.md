> Create curated digests of agent conversations.

Agent-relay-digest is a tool that creates curated summaries or digests of conversations between AI agents. It helps users monitor, analyze, or document agent interactions by generating concise overviews. The project likely processes conversation logs to extract key points and generate readable reports. This can be useful for debugging, evaluation, or maintaining records of multi-agent systems.
