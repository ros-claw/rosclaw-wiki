LatteReview is a low-code Python package designed to simplify and automate systematic literature reviews. It leverages large language models to accelerate the process of screening, extracting, and synthesizing research articles. The tool allows researchers to conduct reviews with minimal coding, making it accessible to non-experts. It integrates with popular LLM APIs and supports structured review workflows. This project is categorized under research-agent systems, emphasizing its role in AI-assisted research automation.
