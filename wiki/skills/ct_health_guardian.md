> Proactive health monitoring for AI agents.

ct-health-guardian is a tool designed for proactive health monitoring of AI agents. It continuously tracks agent metrics and status to detect potential issues before they escalate. This enables early intervention and maintains optimal performance. The system integrates with agent runtimes to collect telemetry data and provide real-time alerts.
