> Automate Outlook: emails, folders, contacts, and calendar integration.

Outlook Automation is a skill within the Composio platform that enables AI agents to programmatically manage Microsoft Outlook tasks. It covers automation for emails, folders, contacts, and calendar integration, allowing agents to send, read, and organize emails, manage contacts, and handle calendar events. This integration helps streamline workflows by reducing manual email and scheduling operations.
