> ☁️ 🍎 🪟 - Seamlessly and securely connect Claude Desktop and other MCP hosts to your favorite apps (Notion, Slack, Monday, Airtable, etc.). Takes less than 90 secs.

WayStation is an aggregator server that connects Claude Desktop and other MCP hosts to popular SaaS applications like Notion, Slack, Monday.com, and Airtable. It enables seamless and secure integration, allowing users to interact with these apps through natural language. Setup takes less than 90 seconds, making it quick to deploy. The tool acts as a bridge between MCP clients and external services, simplifying automation and workflow tasks.
