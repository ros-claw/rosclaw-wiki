> Network-wide ad and tracker blocking at the DNS level.

adblock-dns is a tool that blocks advertisements and trackers across an entire network by filtering DNS queries. It operates at the DNS level, preventing domains known for ads and tracking from being resolved. This provides a lightweight, system-wide solution without needing per-device software. It is analogous to services like Pi-hole but may be designed for specific environments such as AI-agent deployments.
