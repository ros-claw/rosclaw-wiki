> Human Judgment as a Service for AI agents.

askhuman is a service that provides human judgment on demand for AI agents. It allows AI systems to request human input for tasks requiring nuanced decision-making or oversight. This bridges the gap between automated AI and human reasoning, ensuring higher quality outcomes. The service likely operates as a platform connecting human evaluators to AI workflows in real time.
