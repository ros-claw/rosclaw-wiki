OmegaWiki is a wiki-centric research platform built on Claude Code that automates the entire research lifecycle, from literature ingestion and idea generation to experiment design, execution, and paper writing. It implements Andrej Karpathy's LLM-Wiki vision by using a structured knowledge wiki with an interactive graph to store and navigate research state. The platform offers over 20 skills organized into a full loop: ingest, ideate, novelty check, experiment design, experiment run, experiment evaluation, and paper writing. This allows researchers to manage complex projects with an integrated, AI-assisted workflow that keeps all research artifacts in a connected, editable wiki format.
