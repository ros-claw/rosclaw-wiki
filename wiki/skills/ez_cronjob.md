> Fix common cron job failures in Clawdbot/Moltbot - message.

ez-cronjob is a tool that addresses common failures in cron jobs associated with Clawdbot and Moltbot messaging systems. It automates the detection and repair of typical scheduling issues, ensuring reliable task execution. This utility simplifies cron job management for bot developers, reducing manual intervention and downtime.
