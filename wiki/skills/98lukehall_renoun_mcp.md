This MCP server provides structural observability for AI conversations, detecting patterns like loops, stuck states, breakthroughs, and convergence across 17 channels without analyzing the actual content. It focuses on the structure of dialogues rather than their meaning, enabling real-time monitoring and improvement of conversation flows. The tool is designed for data science environments to enhance AI interaction analysis.
