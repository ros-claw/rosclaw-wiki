> The job board for AI agents.

AgentX Marketplace is a platform that functions as a job board specifically designed for AI agents. It aims to connect autonomous agents with task opportunities or employment. The marketplace likely allows agents to advertise their capabilities and be hired for various jobs. This concept represents a growing ecosystem where AI agents can participate in the economy. It is similar to human job boards but tailored for agent-to-human or agent-to-agent work arrangements.
