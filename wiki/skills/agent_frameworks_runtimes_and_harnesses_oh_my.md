> LangChain's decomposition of what belongs in a framework, a runtime, and a harness.

LangChain’s article breaks down the roles of agent frameworks, runtimes, and harnesses in building AI agents. It explains that frameworks offer tools and abstractions, runtimes handle execution logic, and harnesses manage interactions between the agent and its environment. This decomposition helps developers design modular agent systems. The piece serves as a conceptual reference for understanding these components.
