Creative Thought Partner is a conversational AI designed to assist with creative thinking and ideation. It engages users in dialogue to stimulate new ideas and perspectives. The tool leverages natural language processing to facilitate brainstorming sessions. It aims to serve as a digital thought partner for creative professionals.
