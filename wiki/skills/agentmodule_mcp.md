AgentModule/mcp provides an agent-native knowledge infrastructure, offering deterministic and vertical-specific knowledge bases designed for use by autonomous agents through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It includes an integrated ethics module with 22 sub-modules aligned with the EU AI Act, available with every membership. A free 24-hour ethics trial is offered. The service emphasizes reliable, context-aware knowledge delivery tailored to agent consumption.
