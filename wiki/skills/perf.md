> Performance analysis and optimization. Identify bottlenecks and improve speed.

Perf is a performance analysis and optimization plugin that helps developers identify bottlenecks in their code and improve execution speed. It measures various performance metrics to pinpoint slow sections and guides users in applying optimizations. Classified under DevOps and Performance, it integrates into development workflows to enhance overall efficiency.
