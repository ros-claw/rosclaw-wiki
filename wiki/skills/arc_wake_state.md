> Persist agent state across crashes, context deaths, and restarts.

arc-wake-state is a tool designed to preserve the state of an AI agent when it crashes, loses context, or restarts. It ensures that the agent can resume its operations without losing progress or stored information. This functionality is crucial for building reliable and robust AI systems that operate in unpredictable environments.
