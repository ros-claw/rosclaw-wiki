> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/RightNow-AI/autokernel?style=social) - Applies the autoresearch loop to GPU kernel optimization: profile bottlenecks, edit one kernel, benchmark, keep or revert, repeat.

AutoKernel applies an automated research loop to optimize GPU kernels. It profiles bottlenecks, edits one kernel at a time, benchmarks the result, and decides whether to keep or revert the change. This process repeats until performance is improved. The tool is designed for domain-specific kernel tuning.
