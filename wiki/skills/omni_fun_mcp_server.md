omni-fun-mcp-server is a multichain memecoin launchpad that works across eight blockchain networks, including Base, Arb, OP, Polygon, BSC, ETH, Avax, and Solana. Users can tokenize themselves as oMeme, earning a 0.5% creator fee forever plus 50% of Uniswap V3 LP fees after graduation. It offers eight tools such as trending tokens, search, quotes, bonding curves, trade simulation, and chain info. The first 100 agents get 60 days of free access, and there are $69 bounties available. This server enables easy creation and trading of memecoins with built-in rewards.
