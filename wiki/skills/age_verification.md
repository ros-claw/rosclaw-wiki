> Skills for age verification and age-appropriate content filtering.

This project provides skills for age verification in AI agents and tools, ensuring users meet minimum age requirements before accessing content. It also implements filtering mechanisms to present age-appropriate material, protecting vulnerable users from harmful or inappropriate content. These capabilities are essential for compliance with child safety regulations and for building trustworthy AI systems that respect user age.
