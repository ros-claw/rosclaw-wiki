> 🐍 ☁️ - Give your AI assistant its own AI assistants. For example: "Could you ask openai to generate an image of a dog?"

This MCP server allows an AI assistant to delegate tasks to other AI assistants or services, such as asking OpenAI to generate an image. It enables the primary AI to call external APIs for tasks like image generation, expanding its capabilities. The server facilitates multi-agent orchestration by outsourcing specific actions to other AI models or tools.
