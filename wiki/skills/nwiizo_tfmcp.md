> 🦀 🏠 - A Terraform MCP server allowing AI assistants to manage and operate Terraform environments, enabling reading configurations, analyzing plans, applying configurations, and managing Terraform state.

A Rust-based Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that integrates AI assistants with Terraform, enabling reading, planning, applying, and state management of Terraform configurations. It allows AI assistants to analyze Terraform plans and apply changes, acting as a bridge between language models and infrastructure management. The server is designed for cloud platforms and helps automate Terraform operations through natural language interactions.
