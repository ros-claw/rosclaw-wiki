> A position paper that treats the harness layer as a first-class research object, proposes the

This position paper argues that the harness layer—the component responsible for controlling, managing, and interfacing with language agents—should be treated as a first-class research object rather than an afterthought. The authors propose a formal framework for designing harness layers that separate control logic, agency, and runtime concerns. By systematically engineering this layer, agent systems can become more robust, interpretable, and adaptable. The paper contributes a taxonomy of harness functions and discusses implications for future agent architectures.
