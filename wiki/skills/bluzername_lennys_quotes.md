> 📇 🏠 - Query 269 episodes of Lenny's Podcast for product management wisdom. Search 51,000+ transcript segments with YouTube timestamps. Perfect for PRDs, strategy, and PM career advice.

This project indexes over 269 episodes of Lenny's Podcast and enables semantic search across 51,000+ transcript segments, each with YouTube timestamps. It helps product managers quickly extract wisdom on PRDs, strategy, and career advice by querying the entire podcast library. The tool aims to streamline product management research and knowledge retrieval from a popular industry podcast.
