> CLI for AI agents to lookup country info for their humans.

This project provides a command-line tool designed for AI agents to retrieve information about countries on behalf of human users. It simplifies the process of looking up country data such as names, capitals, and codes. The tool is intended to be integrated into AI workflows, enabling automated responses to user queries. It focuses on accurate and quick retrieval of country-related facts.
