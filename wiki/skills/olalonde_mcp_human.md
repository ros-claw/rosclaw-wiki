> 📇 ☁️ - When your LLM needs human assistance (through AWS Mechanical Turk)

This MCP server enables large language models (LLMs) to request human assistance through Amazon Mechanical Turk (MTurk). It acts as a bridge between AI agents and human workers, allowing models to outsource tasks they cannot complete on their own. This facilitates human-in-the-loop workflows where AI can delegate complex or ambiguous tasks to real people. The tool is listed under server implementations for the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
