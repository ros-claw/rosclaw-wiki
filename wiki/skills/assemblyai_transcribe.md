> Transcribe audio/video with AssemblyAI.

This project provides a tool for transcribing audio and video files using the AssemblyAI API. It simplifies the process of converting spoken content into text for further processing. The tool is designed to integrate with AI-agent workflows and embodied intelligence systems. Users can leverage AssemblyAI's speech recognition capabilities without complex setup.
