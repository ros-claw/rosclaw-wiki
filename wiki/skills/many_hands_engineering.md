> A handbook framing the layer above the per-agent harness: how multiple harnessed agents share a commons, where decisions belong on a planned / emergent spectrum, and how human stewardship operates at a different cadence than agent execution. Treats harness engineering as a critical layer of "terrain" the framework sits on top of.

Many Hands Engineering is a handbook that focuses on the layer above per-agent harnesses, explaining how multiple harnessed agents share a commons and how decisions fall on a planned-to-emergent spectrum. It discusses the distinct cadence of human stewardship compared to agent execution and treats harness engineering as a critical terrain that frameworks sit on top of. This work is categorized under Foundations.
