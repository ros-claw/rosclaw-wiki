Legiscan-MCP is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that provides access to legislative data from all 50 US states and the US Congress. It enables users to search bills, retrieve full bill texts, track votes, and look up legislators through the LegiScan API. This tool is designed to integrate legislative information into AI agent workflows, making it useful for research and policy analysis.
