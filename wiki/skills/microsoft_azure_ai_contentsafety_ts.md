This is a TypeScript SDK for Azure AI Content Safety, enabling developers to integrate content moderation into their applications. It provides APIs to detect and filter harmful content such as hate speech, violence, and adult material. The SDK simplifies calling Azure's content safety services from TypeScript or JavaScript projects. It is part of Microsoft's Azure AI offerings under the sponsors category for TypeScript skills.
