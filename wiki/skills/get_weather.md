> Fetch current weather and forecast data from a free weather API.

get-weather is a tool that retrieves current weather conditions and forecasts from a free weather API. It provides a simple interface for integrating weather data into applications or AI agents, supporting location-based queries. The tool can be used standalone or as part of a larger system for real-time environmental information.
