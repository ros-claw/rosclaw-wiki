> Floating avatar widget for AI agents showing emotions, actions.

claw-face is a floating avatar widget designed for AI agents. It visually represents the agent's emotions and actions during interactions. This enhances user engagement by giving AI a more expressive, human-like presence. The widget can be integrated into platforms to make AI interactions more intuitive and enjoyable.
