Terraform Stacks is a feature by HashiCorp that enables managing multiple Terraform configurations as a single unit, allowing for hierarchical deployment and orchestration of infrastructure at scale. It simplifies complex multi-environment setups by defining stacks as reusable modules with dependencies and outputs. This tool integrates with Terraform Cloud and Enterprise to provide collaborative infrastructure management. It's designed for teams that need to coordinate infrastructure across many projects or environments efficiently.
