> Automate Sentry: issues, events, projects, releases, and alerts.

Sentry Automation is a skill that integrates with Composio to automate common operations in Sentry, including managing issues, events, projects, releases, and alerts. It allows developers to trigger automated actions based on Sentry events, reducing manual workload and enabling faster incident response. The integration supports workflows for continuous monitoring and error tracking.
