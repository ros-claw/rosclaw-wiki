> Secure secret handoff from human to AI.

Confidant is a tool for securely handing off secrets from humans to AI agents. It focuses on secure transfer of sensitive information like API keys or passwords to AI systems. The project aims to enable trusted communication of secrets in a way that maintains confidentiality and integrity. It likely uses encryption or other security mechanisms to protect the secret during handoff.
