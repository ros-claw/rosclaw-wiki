> CAPA system management for medical device QMS.

capa-officer is a software tool for managing Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) systems within a medical device Quality Management System (QMS). It helps organizations track, document, and resolve quality issues to maintain regulatory compliance. The tool streamlines CAPA workflows, supports audit readiness, and integrates with other QMS components.
