> Three-phase research pipeline: Literature Review → Experimentation → Report Writing, with specialized agents for each phase.

Agent Laboratory is a system that automates research through a three-phase pipeline: literature review, experimentation, and report writing. Specialized AI agents handle each phase, reducing manual effort and speeding up the research process. It enables efficient and thorough research workflows by leveraging AI capabilities. This tool is designed for researchers to streamline their work and accelerate outcomes.
