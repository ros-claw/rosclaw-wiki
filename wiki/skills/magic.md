> A benchmark measuring cognition, adaptability, rationality, and collaboration of LLMs in multi-agent systems, useful for evaluating how harnesses coordinate agent interactions and shared state.

MAgIC is a benchmark designed to evaluate multi-agent systems that use large language models. It measures agents' cognition, adaptability, rationality, and collaboration. The benchmark is particularly useful for assessing how harnesses coordinate agent interactions and manage shared state. It falls under the category of benchmarks for embodied intelligence and AI agent tooling.
