> Schedule social media posts and threads across 28+ platforms.

Postiz is a tool that allows users to schedule social media posts and threads across over 28 different platforms. It simplifies managing multiple social media accounts by automating the posting process. Users can plan and organize their content calendar in advance. This helps maintain a consistent online presence without manual posting on each platform.
