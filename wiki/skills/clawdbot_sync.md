> Synchronize memory, preferences, and skills between multiple.

clawdbot-sync is a tool designed to synchronize memory, preferences, and skills across multiple bot instances. It ensures that all instances share consistent state, enabling seamless user experiences and collaboration between agents. The synchronization process likely handles conflict resolution, versioning, and network challenges. This tool is valuable for scaling bots, maintaining persistent context, and supporting multi-agent systems.
