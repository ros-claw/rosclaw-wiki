> RAM-safe voice transcription with auto-chunking — works on 16GB machines without crashes.

auto-whisper-safe is a voice transcription tool that prevents out-of-memory crashes on systems with 16GB of RAM by automatically chunking audio input. It leverages the Whisper speech recognition model while dividing long files into manageable segments. This approach ensures stable and reliable transcription without exceeding memory limits, making it accessible to users with moderate hardware.
