> Claude Code skill that automates mountain route research for North American peaks. Aggregates data from 10+ mountaineering sources like Mountaineers.org, PeakBagger.com and SummitPost.com to generate detailed route beta reports with weather, avalanche conditions, and trip reports.

This Claude Code skill automates mountain route research for North American peaks by aggregating data from over 10 mountaineering sources. It generates detailed route beta reports that include weather conditions, avalanche forecasts, and trip reports. The skill streamlines the planning process for climbers by consolidating information from multiple platforms. It is categorized under Agent Skills > General on the platform.
