> Issue ClawPrint reverse-CAPTCHA challenges to verify.

Captcha-ai is a tool that issues reverse-CAPTCHA challenges using a method called ClawPrint to verify users or agents. These challenges are designed to be difficult for automated systems, serving as a form of Turing test for authentication. The tool likely integrates with AI systems for bot detection or human verification in automated pipelines.
