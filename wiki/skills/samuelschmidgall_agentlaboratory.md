> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/SamuelSchmidgall/AgentLaboratory?style=social) - End-to-end autonomous research workflow: idea → literature review → experiments → report. Supports both autonomous and co-pilot modes.

AgentLaboratory is an end-to-end autonomous research workflow that guides AI agents through the entire research process from idea generation to report writing. It supports both fully autonomous and co-pilot (human-interactive) modes, allowing researchers to collaborate with AI at each step. The system automates literature reviews, experiment design, execution, and paper drafting, aiming to accelerate scientific discovery.
