FinanceKit-MCP is an MCP server that delivers financial market intelligence through 17 tools. It provides stock quotes, technical analysis indicators like RSI and MACD, and crypto prices via CoinGecko. The server also offers risk metrics such as VaR and Sharpe ratio, options chains, earnings calendar, sector rotation analysis, and portfolio analysis. It is designed for financial analysts and developers needing programmatic access to market data and analytics.
