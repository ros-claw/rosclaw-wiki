> Trigger Alter macOS app actions via x-callback-urls.

Alter-actions enables users to trigger specific actions within the Alter macOS application by utilizing x-callback-urls, a standard for inter-app communication on Apple platforms. This allows for automation and integration of Alter app functions with other tools, scripts, or workflows. By calling a URL with predefined parameters, users can execute actions like creating notes or performing searches without manual intervention.
