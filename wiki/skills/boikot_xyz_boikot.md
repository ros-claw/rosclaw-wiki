> 🦀☁️ - Model Context Protocol Server for looking up company ethics information. Learn about the ethical and unethical actions of major companies.

Boikot is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that provides information about company ethics, allowing users to look up ethical and unethical actions of major corporations. It enables AI agents and applications to access structured data on company behavior, such as boycotts or controversies. The tool is designed for search and data extraction, integrating with MCP-compatible clients to deliver real-time ethical assessments.
