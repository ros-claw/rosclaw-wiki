Humanize Chinese is a tool that converts machine-generated Chinese text into more natural, human-like writing. It improves the fluency and authenticity of Chinese content produced by AI agents or language models. Listed as a community contributor to an embodied-intelligence project, it helps make AI-generated Chinese less robotic and more culturally appropriate. This supports natural interactions between humans and AI agents in Chinese-speaking contexts.
