> 📇 🏠 - Enhanced graph-based memory with a focus on AI role-play and story generation

MemoryMesh is an enhanced graph-based memory system designed to improve AI role-play and story generation. It organizes information as a mesh of interconnected nodes, enabling more coherent retrieval and recall. This approach helps AI agents maintain long-term context and narrative consistency.
