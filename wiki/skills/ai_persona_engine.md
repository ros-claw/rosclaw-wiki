> Build emotionally intelligent AI personas for voice and chat roleplay using actor-direction prompts instead.

AI Persona Engine enables developers to build emotionally intelligent AI personas for voice and chat roleplay. Instead of traditional system prompts, it uses actor-direction prompts to guide AI behavior. This approach allows for more nuanced and realistic character interactions. The engine is designed for roleplay scenarios where emotional depth and consistency are important.
