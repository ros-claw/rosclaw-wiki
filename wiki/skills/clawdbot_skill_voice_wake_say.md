> Speak responses aloud on macOS.

This skill adds voice output capability to the ClawDBot assistant on macOS systems. It uses macOS's built-in text-to-speech to speak responses aloud. The skill can be used for hands-free interaction or accessibility purposes. It is designed as a plugin for the ClawDBot framework.
