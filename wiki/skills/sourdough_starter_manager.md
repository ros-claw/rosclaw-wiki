> Manage sourdough starters with feeding schedules, hydration calculations, health tracking, and baking preparation.

This tool helps bakers manage sourdough starters by tracking feeding schedules, calculating hydration ratios, monitoring starter health, and preparing for baking. It simplifies the maintenance of wild yeast cultures. Users can log feedings and receive reminders. It's useful for both hobbyists and professional bakers.
