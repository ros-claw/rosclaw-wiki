Audits MCP servers for vulnerabilities, detects prompt injection, infinite loops, token bombing, and missing human oversight across 20+ frameworks. Maps findings to EU AI Act, OWASP LLM Top 10.

inkog-mcp is an AI agent security scanner specifically designed for auditing MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers. It detects vulnerabilities such as prompt injection, infinite loops, token bombing, and missing human oversight across more than 20 frameworks. The tool also maps its findings to regulatory and industry standards like the EU AI Act and OWASP LLM Top 10, helping developers ensure their AI agent implementations are secure and compliant.
