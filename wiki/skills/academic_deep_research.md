> Transparent, rigorous research with full.

Academic-deep-research is a tool for conducting rigorous academic research with full transparency. It systematically gathers and cites sources to produce comprehensive literature reviews. The agent aims to provide verifiable and reproducible research outputs. This approach ensures that all reasoning and evidence are traceable. It is designed for researchers who demand thoroughness and openness in automated research.
