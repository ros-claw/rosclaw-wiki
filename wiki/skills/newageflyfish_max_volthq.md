Compare inference pricing across 8 providers in real time with routing recommendations and spend tracking. One-command install: `npx volthq-mcp-server --setup`.

Volthq is a compute price oracle designed for AI agents, providing real-time comparison of inference pricing across 8 different providers. It offers routing recommendations to optimize costs and includes spend tracking capabilities. The MCP server can be installed with a single command (`npx volthq-mcp-server --setup`) and is cross-platform, supporting various operating systems. It helps AI agents make cost-efficient decisions when selecting inference providers.
