> HSM-backed vault for agent secrets; store, rotate, share securely.

1claw is an HSM-backed vault designed for securely storing, rotating, and sharing secrets used by AI agents. It leverages hardware security modules to provide strong encryption and protection for sensitive credentials. The tool enables automated secret rotation to reduce risk and supports controlled sharing among different agents or services. This allows development teams to manage agent secrets with high security standards without manual overhead.
