Idea-OS is an open-source platform designed to facilitate the collection, organization, and development of ideas. It provides tools for collaborative brainstorming, idea evaluation, and tracking progress from conception to implementation. The project aims to support innovation management within teams or communities by offering a structured environment for idea cultivation.
