> 📇 ☁️ - Email discovery, verification, and enrichment tools. Find email addresses, verify deliverability, enrich contact data, discover authors and LinkedIn profiles, validate phone numbers, and analyze technology stacks.

The tomba-mcp-server integrates Tomba's email discovery and enrichment services into the Model Context Protocol. It allows AI agents to find and verify email addresses, enrich contact data, discover LinkedIn profiles, validate phone numbers, and analyze technology stacks. This server is listed under marketing tools and simplifies tasks like lead generation and contact enrichment through a standardized interface.
