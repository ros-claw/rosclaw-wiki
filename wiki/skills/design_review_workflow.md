> A tailored workflow for enabling automated UI/UX design review, including specialized sub agents, slash commands, `CLAUDE.md` excerpts, and more. Covers a broad range of criteria from responsive design to accessibility.

This workflow automates UI/UX design reviews using specialized sub-agents and slash commands. It includes CLAUDE.md excerpts and covers a broad range of criteria from responsive design to accessibility. The aim is to streamline the design review process for development teams, ensuring consistent and thorough evaluations.
