> Search and browse local Beeper chat history.

Beeper is a tool designed to search and browse local Beeper chat history. It allows users to index their chat logs stored on their device for quick retrieval. This enables offline access to past conversations without relying on Beeper's cloud search. The tool is useful for power users who want granular control over their messaging data.
