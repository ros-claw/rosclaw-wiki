This article presents a three-way taxonomy of codebases that AI agents encounter: agent-native greenfield, true legacy brownfield, and recently-vibecoded inheritance. It offers practical playbooks for managing these codebases using layered CLAUDE.md rules, ratcheted pre-commit hooks, baselined lint violations, and feature-folder refactors. The overarching goal is to eliminate the codebase itself as a bottleneck in the agent harness, enabling smoother integration and maintenance.
