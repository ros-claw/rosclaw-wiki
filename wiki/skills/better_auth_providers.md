better-auth/providers is a package that offers a collection of authentication providers for the Better Auth framework. It supports various OAuth and single sign-on (SSO) integrations, making it easier to add multiple login options to web applications. The project is maintained by the Better Auth Team and is listed under their sponsored skills. It simplifies the process of connecting to external identity providers.
