This MCP server connects AI agents to a global database of government procurement opportunities, including tenders, RFPs, grants, and frameworks. It aggregates data from over 25 official sources worldwide, enabling users to automatically search and match their products or consulting services to relevant live opportunities. The server simplifies the process of finding and bidding on government contracts, making it easier for businesses to discover new revenue streams.
