> A text-based calendar and scheduling application.

Calcurse is a lightweight terminal-based calendar and scheduling application for Unix-like systems. It offers an interactive interface for managing events, todos, and appointments. Users can navigate with keyboard shortcuts and sync with CalDAV servers. It supports recurring events, color themes, and custom alarms. Calcurse is designed for efficiency and minimal resource usage.
