> Create, discover, and manage events with NFT tickets on the Belong platform.

Belong-events is a platform that enables users to create, discover, and manage events using NFT tickets. Event organizers can issue NFT-based tickets, providing verifiable ownership and transferability on the blockchain. Attendees can find and purchase tickets through the Belong platform. This integrates event management with Web3 technology for enhanced security and interoperability.
