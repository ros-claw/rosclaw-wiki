> Full CRUD for Notion pages, databases.

better-notion is a Python library that provides full CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) functionality for Notion pages and databases. It offers a convenient interface to interact with the Notion API, making it easier to manage Notion data programmatically. The library is designed for developers who want to integrate Notion with their Python applications.
