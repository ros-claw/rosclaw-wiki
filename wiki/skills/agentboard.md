> A benchmark for multi-turn LLM agents complemented by an analytical evaluation board for assessing model performance beyond final success rates, making partial-progress and trajectory quality visible.

AgentBoard is a benchmark designed for multi-turn LLM agents. It includes an analytical evaluation board that provides insights beyond simple success rates. The board makes visible partial progress and the quality of the agent's trajectory. This allows for a more nuanced assessment of agent performance.
