> Set up and maintain basic bookkeeping for a solopreneur.

This project provides guidance on setting up and maintaining basic bookkeeping for a solopreneur. It covers fundamental accounting practices, expense tracking, income recording, and financial organization. Aimed at independent business owners who need simple bookkeeping without complex software. The goal is to help solopreneurs manage their finances efficiently and stay compliant.
