> 🎖️ 🦀 🏠 🍎 Local-first system capturing screen/audio with timestamped indexing, SQL/embedding storage, semantic search, LLM-powered history analysis, and event-triggered actions - enables building context-aware AI agents through a NextJS plugin ecosystem.

Screenpipe is a local-first system that captures screen and audio data, indexing it with timestamps, SQL, and embeddings for semantic search. It uses LLMs to analyze history and trigger actions based on events, enabling the creation of context-aware AI agents. The tool integrates with a NextJS plugin ecosystem for extensibility.
