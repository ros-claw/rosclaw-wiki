> [![coremcp](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/CoreBaseHQ/coremcp/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/CoreBaseHQ/coremcp) 🏎️ ☁️ 🏠 - A secure, tunnel-native database bridge for AI agents. Connects localhost & on-premise databases (MSSQL, etc.) to LLMs with AST-based query safety and PII masking.

CoreMCP is a secure, tunnel-native database bridge that enables AI agents to connect to localhost and on-premise databases, including MSSQL. It provides AST-based query safety and PII masking to protect sensitive data. The bridge facilitates seamless communication between databases and LLMs while maintaining security.
