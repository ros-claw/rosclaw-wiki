This document provides guidance on using workflows for outcome-driven execution within the Bundles & Workflows framework. It focuses on creating human-readable playbooks that define sequences of actions to achieve specific goals. The workflows are designed to be modular, reusable, and clear for both human operators and AI agents. By structuring tasks as workflows, users can ensure predictable, auditable outcomes in embodied-intelligence and agent-based systems.
