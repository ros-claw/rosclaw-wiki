> Save any product to a universal wishlist.

add-wish is a tool that lets users save any product to a universal wishlist, consolidating desired items from various online stores into one central place. It simplifies tracking and managing wishlists across different platforms, making it easier to keep tabs on products users want to purchase or monitor. By providing a universal list, it eliminates the need for separate wishlists on each retailer's site.
