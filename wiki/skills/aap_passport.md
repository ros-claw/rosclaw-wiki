> Agent Attestation Protocol - The Reverse Turing Test.

The Agent Attestation Protocol (AAP) introduces a reverse Turing test to verify human identity online. It provides a passport-like credential for agents to prove they are not automated. This protocol aims to combat AI impersonation and establish trust in digital interactions. The approach likely involves cryptographic challenges that require human cognition or biometric verification.
