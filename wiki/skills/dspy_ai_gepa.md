> DSPy integration of GEPA reflective prompt optimizer for compound AI systems.

This project integrates GEPA, a reflective prompt optimizer, into the DSPy framework for compound AI systems. It allows DSPy users to leverage GEPA's optimization techniques to automatically improve prompts for multi-step AI pipelines. The integration enhances the ability to refine prompts through reflection and iterative feedback. This tool is designed for developers building complex AI systems that require prompt optimization.
