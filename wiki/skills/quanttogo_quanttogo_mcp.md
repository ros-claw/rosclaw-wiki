Covers US and A-Share markets with forward-tracked performance.

This MCP server provides quantitative trading signals based on macro factors for AI agents. It offers eight tools for discovering trading strategies, retrieving live signals, and registering for trials. The service covers both US and Chinese A-Share markets. Performance data is forward-tracked, meaning the signals are based on historical predictions rather than backward-looking analysis. It enables AI agents to integrate real-time financial signals into their decision-making.
