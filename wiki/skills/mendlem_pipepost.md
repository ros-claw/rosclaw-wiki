Drafts SEO-scored articles, cross-publishes to Dev.to, Ghost, Hashnode, WordPress, and Medium with auto-wired canonical URLs, generates social posts for Twitter/LinkedIn/Reddit/Bluesky/HN, fetches Unsplash cover images, and submits URLs to IndexNow — all as local stdio, no cloud relay.

Pipepost is an MCP server that allows users to draft SEO-optimized articles directly from the terminal. It can cross-publish content to multiple platforms including Dev.to, Ghost, Hashnode, WordPress, and Medium, while automatically setting canonical URLs to avoid duplicate content issues. The tool also generates social media posts for Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Bluesky, and Hacker News, fetches cover images from Unsplash, and submits URLs to IndexNow for fast indexing. It runs as a local stdio server with no cloud relay, ensuring full privacy and control.
