> Track any detox with customizable counters, symptom logging.

detox-counter is a tool for tracking any kind of detox regimen using customizable counters and symptom logging. Users can monitor progress by counting days, tasks, or other metrics, and record any symptoms they experience. It provides a structured way to manage detox periods and observe health changes over time.
