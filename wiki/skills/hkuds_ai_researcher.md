> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/HKUDS/AI-Researcher?style=social) - NeurIPS 2025 paper. Full end-to-end research automation: hypothesis → experiments → manuscript → peer review. Production version at [novix.science](https://novix.science/chat).

AI-Researcher is an end-to-end research automation system that handles the entire scientific process from hypothesis generation to peer review. It was accepted at NeurIPS 2025 and has a production version available at novix.science. The system enables fully autonomous research without human intervention.
