> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/vukrosic/auto-research?style=social) - Docs-only control plane for an open autonomous AI research lab — file-based operating model for human direction and agent execution.

auto-research is an open-source project that provides a docs-only control plane for an autonomous AI research lab. It uses a file-based operating model where human instructions and agent actions are managed through documents. This approach allows researchers to direct AI agents by writing and updating documentation, which the system then interprets and executes. The goal is to create a transparent and structured way to conduct AI research with both human oversight and agent autonomy.
