> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/iii-hq/n-autoresearch?style=social) - Multi-GPU autoresearch infrastructure with structured experiment tracking, adaptive search strategy, crash recovery, and queryable orchestration around the classic `train.py` loop.

n-autoresearch is a multi-GPU infrastructure designed to automate machine learning research. It offers structured experiment tracking, adaptive search strategies, and crash recovery to streamline the classic train.py training loop. The system enables queryable orchestration of experiments, making it robust for large-scale, distributed training. Its goal is to simplify resource management and accelerate research workflows.
