Raw official records, no intermediaries. A platform by sophymarine. Hosted at `openregistry.sophymarine.com/mcp` — OAuth 2.1, streamable HTTP, free anonymous tier.

This MCP server provides live, official data from 27 national company registries, including UK Companies House, France RNE, Germany Handelsregister, Korea OpenDART, Canada CBCA, and 10 US states. It is designed for KYC, AML, and due-diligence workflows, offering raw official records without intermediaries. The server is hosted at openregistry.sophymarine.com/mcp, supports OAuth 2.1 and streamable HTTP, and includes a free anonymous tier.
