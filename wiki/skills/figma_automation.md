> Automate Figma: files, components, comments, projects, and team management.

This tool allows users to automate various Figma tasks such as managing files, components, comments, projects, and teams. It is categorized under App Automation via Composio, a platform that connects applications for workflow automation. By leveraging Figma's API, it enables programmatic control over design assets and collaboration features. This automation helps designers and teams streamline repetitive tasks and integrate Figma with other tools in their stack.
