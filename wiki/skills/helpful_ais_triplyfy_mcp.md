> 📇 ☁️ - An MCP server that lets LLMs plan and manage itineraries with interactive maps in Triplyfy; manage itineraries, places and notes, and search/save flights.

This MCP server enables large language models to plan and manage travel itineraries through the Triplyfy platform. It supports creating and editing itineraries, adding places and notes, and searching or saving flights. Interactive maps are integrated to visualize travel plans. The server is categorized under travel and transportation, and is designed to work with MCP-compatible AI agents.
