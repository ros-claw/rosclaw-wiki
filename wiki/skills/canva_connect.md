> Manage Canva designs, assets, and folders via the Connect API.

canva-connect is a tool that leverages Canva's Connect API to manage designs, assets, and folders programmatically. It enables users to automate workflows for creating, updating, and organizing Canva resources without manual intervention. This integration is useful for developers and teams looking to embed Canva capabilities into their own applications or automate design tasks.
