> AI management middleware with 14 mentors and 9 culture packs.

boss-ai-agent is an AI management middleware designed to govern and guide AI agent behavior within organizations. It provides 14 predefined mentor profiles and 9 culture packs to align AI actions with company values and norms. This tool helps enterprises integrate AI systems while maintaining cultural consistency and oversight. The middleware likely interfaces with existing AI platforms to enforce rules and provide contextual guidance.
