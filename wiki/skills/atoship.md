> Ship packages with AI — compare rates across USPS, FedEx, and UPS, buy discounted labels, track shipments.

Atoship is an AI-powered shipping platform that helps users compare parcel rates across USPS, FedEx, and UPS. It enables purchasing discounted shipping labels directly and provides shipment tracking capabilities. The service aims to simplify logistics for small businesses and individuals by leveraging AI to find the best rates.
