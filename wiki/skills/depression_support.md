> Daily support for depression with mood tracking.

This project focuses on providing daily support for individuals experiencing depression. It incorporates mood tracking to help users monitor their emotional well-being over time. The tool aims to offer consistent encouragement and insights into mood patterns. It may also suggest coping strategies or resources for mental health management.
