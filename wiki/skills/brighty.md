> Banking interface for AI bots and automation.

Brighty is a banking interface tailored for AI bots and automation systems, enabling automated financial transactions and account management. It provides APIs and tools that allow AI agents to interact with banking services programmatically. The platform abstracts complex banking operations into simple commands for bots. This enables seamless integration of financial capabilities into AI workflows and automated processes.
