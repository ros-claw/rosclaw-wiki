> Productivity system based on Maya calendar with 13 natural tones for project management and personal development.

This project introduces a productivity system inspired by the Maya calendar, using 13 natural tones to structure sprints. It is designed for project management and personal development, breaking work into rhythmic 13-day cycles. The method aims to align productivity with natural cycles for improved focus and creativity. It offers a unique alternative to traditional time management approaches.
