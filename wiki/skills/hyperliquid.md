> Read-only Hyperliquid market data assistant (perps + spot optional)

Hyperliquid is a read-only assistant for fetching market data from the Hyperliquid exchange. It supports perpetuals (perps) and optionally spot markets. The tool provides access to market data such as prices, order books, and other relevant information without write capabilities.
