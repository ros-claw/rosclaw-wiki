This MCP server provides risk scores and asset holdings for EVM blockchain addresses, including externally owned accounts (EOAs), contract addresses, ENS names, and even domain names. It is designed for finance and fintech applications, enabling risk assessment and portfolio tracking directly from AI agents. The server likely integrates with blockchain data sources to compute scores and retrieve holdings. For researchers in embodied intelligence and AI agent tooling, this offers a way to query blockchain risk and asset information through a standardized interface.
