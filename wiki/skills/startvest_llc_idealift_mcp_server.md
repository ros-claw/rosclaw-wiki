Captures product feedback from Slack, Teams, Discord, and GitHub.

Idealift MCP server is a decision intelligence tool that aggregates product feedback signals from platforms like Slack, Teams, Discord, and GitHub. It applies RICE scoring to prioritize features and maintains audit trails for decisions. This helps teams make data-driven product decisions by centralizing feedback and scoring.
