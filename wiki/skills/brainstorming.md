> Transform rough ideas into fully-formed designs through structured questioning and alternative exploration.

This skill helps users or AI agents transform vague, rough ideas into well-developed designs by employing structured questioning and systematic exploration of alternatives. It guides the brainstorming process to ensure all angles are considered, fostering creativity and thoroughness. The method encourages divergent thinking before converging on a refined final concept. It is useful for problem-solving, product design, and creative projects.
