> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/HKUDS/ClawTeam?style=social) - Agent swarm intelligence for autoresearch — spawns parallel GPU research directions, distributes work across agents, aggregates results.

ClawTeam is an agent swarm intelligence system designed for automated research. It spawns parallel GPU research directions, distributes work across multiple agents, and aggregates the results. This enables efficient exploration of multiple research paths simultaneously, leveraging GPU resources effectively. The system aims to accelerate scientific discovery through collaborative AI agents.
