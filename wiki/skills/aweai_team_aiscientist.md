AiScientist is an open-source research-agent system that automates parts of the scientific method, including hypothesis generation, experiment design, and analysis. It aims to accelerate discovery by acting as an autonomous AI collaborator for researchers. The project is listed under research-agent systems and is designed to be extensible and modular.
