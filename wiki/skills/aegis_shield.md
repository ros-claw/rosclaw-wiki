> Prompt-injection and data-exfiltration screening for untrusted text.

aegis-shield is a tool for screening untrusted text to detect prompt injection attacks and data exfiltration attempts. It helps protect AI systems from malicious inputs that try to manipulate or extract sensitive information. The tool analyzes text before it reaches an AI model to block harmful content. It is designed for use with large language models and other natural language processing systems.
