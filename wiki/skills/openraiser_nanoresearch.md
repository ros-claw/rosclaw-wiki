> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OpenRaiser/NanoResearch?style=social) - End-to-end autonomous research engine that plans experiments, generates code, runs jobs locally or on SLURM, analyzes real results, and writes papers grounded in those outputs.

NanoResearch is an end-to-end autonomous research engine designed to automate the entire scientific research lifecycle. It plans experiments, generates and executes code locally or on SLURM clusters, analyzes real experimental results, and writes research papers grounded in those outputs. The system aims to reduce manual effort in computational research while ensuring reproducibility and rigor. It integrates with computational job schedulers and supports paper generation from analysis.
