> Agents can sign plugins, rotate credentials without losing identity, and publicly attest to behavior.

0protocol enables software agents to cryptographically sign plugins, ensuring trust and integrity. It allows agents to rotate their credentials (e.g., keys) without losing their established identity. Agents can publicly attest to their behavior or actions, providing transparency and accountability. This protocol is designed for decentralized environments where agents need to maintain a consistent, verifiable identity over time.
