> A benchmark for evaluating role-playing conversational agents using multi-turn dialogues and character profiles, with metrics across four dimensions including character fidelity and conversational coherence.

CharacterEval is a benchmark designed to evaluate role-playing conversational agents. It uses multi-turn dialogues and character profiles to assess performance across four dimensions, including character fidelity and conversational coherence. The benchmark provides a standardized way to measure how well AI agents can maintain consistent character roles in conversations.
