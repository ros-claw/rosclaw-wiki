> Comprehensive backup, update, and restore.

clawdbot-skill-update is a tool designed for comprehensive backup, update, and restore operations, specifically for clawdbot skills. It ensures data integrity during skill updates by creating backups before applying changes. The tool also provides a restore mechanism to revert to previous states if needed. This simplifies skill maintenance and reduces the risk of data loss.
