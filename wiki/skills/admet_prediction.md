> ADMET (Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, Toxicity) prediction for drug candidates.

This project focuses on predicting ADMET (Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, Toxicity) properties for drug candidates. It aims to accelerate drug discovery by providing computational assessments of pharmacokinetics and safety. The tool likely leverages machine learning or quantitative structure-activity relationship (QSAR) models. By screening compounds early, it reduces the risk of late-stage failures in drug development.
