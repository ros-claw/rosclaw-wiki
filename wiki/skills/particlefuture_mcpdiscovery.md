> 🐍 ☁️ 🏠 🍎 🪟 - MCP of MCPs. Automatic discovery and configure MCP servers on your local machine.

MCPDiscovery is an aggregator tool that automatically discovers and configures MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers on your local machine. It acts as a 'MCP of MCPs,' streamlining the setup process across multiple platforms including cloud, home, and desktop environments. The project aims to simplify the integration of various MCP servers by handling discovery and configuration automatically.
