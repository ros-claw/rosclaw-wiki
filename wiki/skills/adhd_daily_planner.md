> Time-blind friendly planning, executive function.

This project is a daily planner specifically designed for individuals with ADHD. It addresses time-blindness, a common challenge where people struggle to perceive and estimate time, and supports executive function skills like prioritization and task initiation. The planner likely uses alternative scheduling methods, such as time blocking without strict deadlines, to reduce overwhelm. Its goal is to help users manage daily tasks more effectively by accommodating neurodivergent cognitive styles.
