open-sales-stack is a collection of MCP servers designed for B2B sales intelligence. It provides tools for website analysis, technology stack detection, hiring signals, review aggregation, ad tracking, social profiles, and financial reporting. These capabilities enable AI-powered prospecting and lead generation. The project is maintained by Ekas and supports multiple platforms.
