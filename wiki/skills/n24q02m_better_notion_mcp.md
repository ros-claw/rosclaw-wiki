This server provides a Markdown-first interface to Notion via the Model Context Protocol, offering 9 composite tools and 39 actions for interacting with Notion pages and databases. It achieves approximately 77% token reduction through tiered documentation, and includes auto-pagination and bulk operations for efficient data handling. Designed for AI agents, it streamlines workplace productivity tasks.
