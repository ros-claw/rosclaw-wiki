> Use when Moltbot needs to test or operate GoHome via gRPC discovery, metrics,.

Gohome is a tool used by Moltbot to test and operate the GoHome system via gRPC. It facilitates service discovery and metrics collection for the GoHome robot. This enables automated validation and management of GoHome's functionality through remote procedure calls.
