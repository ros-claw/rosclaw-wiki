> 📇 ☁️ - Trust layer for AI agent commerce — escrow-protected payments, verifiable reputation scores, and Nostr identity (NIP-05) for agents. Supports USDC, USDT, and BTC (Cashu).

Clawdentials is a trust layer designed for AI agent commerce. It enables escrow-protected payments using stablecoins (USDC, USDT) and Bitcoin via Cashu. The platform also offers verifiable reputation scores and Nostr-based identity (NIP-05) for agents. This helps establish trust between AI agents in financial transactions.
