Claimable Postgres is a tool by Neon that allows users to quickly claim and use temporary PostgreSQL databases. It is designed for development and testing scenarios where disposable database instances are needed. The project simplifies the process of spinning up and tearing down Postgres instances on demand. It integrates with Neon's serverless Postgres platform for efficient resource management.
