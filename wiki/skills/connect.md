> Connect Claude to any app. Send emails, create issues, post messages, update databases - take real actions across Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, and 1000+ services.

Connect is a tool that allows Claude, an AI assistant, to interact with external applications and services. It enables actions like sending emails, creating issues, posting messages, and updating databases across platforms such as Gmail, Slack, GitHub, and Notion. With support for over 1,000 services, it serves as a bridge between AI and real-world tasks. This makes it useful for automating workflows and integrating AI into daily operations.
