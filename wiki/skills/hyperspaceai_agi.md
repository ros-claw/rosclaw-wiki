> ![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/hyperspaceai/agi?style=social) - Distributed, peer-to-peer research network where autonomous agents run experiments, gossip findings, maintain CRDT leaderboards, and archive results to GitHub across multiple research domains.

This project creates a distributed, peer-to-peer network where autonomous AI agents conduct research experiments. Agents share findings through gossip protocols and maintain leaderboards using Conflict-free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs). Results are archived to GitHub, enabling collaboration across multiple research domains. The system is designed to scale research by combining collective intelligence with decentralized coordination.
