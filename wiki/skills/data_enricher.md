> Enrich leads with email addresses and format data.

A tool to enrich lead data by appending email addresses and standardizing formatting, useful for sales and marketing automation. It can be integrated into AI agent pipelines for data quality improvement. The data-enricher helps in lead scoring and CRM updates, streamlining the lead qualification process.
