> This skill enables IP address masking and accessing hidden services.

The anyone-proxy skill enables IP address masking and access to hidden services, providing anonymity for users. It routes traffic through proxy servers or anonymity networks like Tor to protect privacy and bypass restrictions. This tool is designed for use within embodied intelligence systems, allowing agents to browse the web securely. It helps in hiding the user's real location and accessing content that may be blocked or hidden.
