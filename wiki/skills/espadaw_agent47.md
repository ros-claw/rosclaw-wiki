> 📇 ☁️ - Unified job aggregator for AI agents across 9+ platforms (x402, RentAHuman, Virtuals, etc).

Agent47 is a unified job aggregator that collects and consolidates task opportunities for AI agents from multiple platforms, including x402, RentAHuman, and Virtuals. It simplifies the process of finding work for AI agents by providing a single access point to diverse marketplaces. The project focuses on integrating server-side aggregation for efficiency and scalability.
