> Fetches and summarizes recent arXiv and Hugging.

This tool fetches recent research papers from arXiv and Hugging Face, then generates concise summaries. It uses an agentic approach, likely employing autonomous AI agents to perform the fetching and summarization tasks automatically. The digest helps researchers quickly stay updated with the latest findings without needing to read full papers. It is aimed at streamlining the process of monitoring new publications in machine learning and AI fields.
