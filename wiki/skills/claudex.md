> Claudex - A web-based browser for exploring your Claude Code conversation history across projects. Indexes your codebase for full-text search. Nice, easy-to-navigate UI. Simple dashboard interface for high-level analytics, and multiple export options as well. (And completely local w/ no telemetry!)

Claudex is a web-based browser that helps you explore and search through your Claude Code conversation history across different projects. It indexes your codebase for full-text search, providing a clean and easy-to-navigate dashboard interface with high-level analytics. The tool offers multiple export options and runs completely locally with no telemetry, ensuring your data stays private. It is designed for developers who want to analyze and revisit their AI code assistant conversations efficiently.
