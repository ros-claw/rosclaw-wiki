Whether or not you practice Ralph-Driven Development (RDD), I think these projects are interesting and creative explorations of general agentic patterns.

Ralph Wiggum Marketer is a Claude Code plugin that functions as an autonomous AI copywriter. It integrates the Ralph loop methodology with customized knowledge bases to empower market research agents. The agents conduct research while the plugin generates copy automatically, minimizing human effort. This project is part of the Ralph-Driven Development (RDD) ecosystem and serves as a creative exploration of general agentic patterns.
