> 🐍 🏠 - Multi-LLM deliberation with anonymized peer review. Runs a 3-stage council: parallel responses → anonymous ranking → synthesis. Based on Andrej Karpathy's LLM Council concept.

LLM Council is a multi-LLM deliberation system that implements a three-stage council process: parallel responses from multiple language models, anonymous ranking of those responses, and synthesis into a final output. It is based on Andrej Karpathy's concept of an LLM council, enabling structured peer review among models. The system aims to improve response quality through collective intelligence and anonymity to reduce bias.
