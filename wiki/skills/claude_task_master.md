> A task management system for AI-driven development with Claude, designed to work seamlessly with Cursor AI.

Claude Task Master is a task management system tailored for AI-driven software development, integrating with Claude AI and Cursor IDE. It helps organize and track development tasks, leveraging AI to streamline workflows. The tool falls under the category of orchestrators, coordinating AI agents and human developers. It aims to enhance productivity by providing a structured approach to managing AI-assisted coding projects.
