> Local calorie logging and visual reporting (auto-refreshes and returns report image after each log)

A local application that logs calorie intake and automatically generates a visual report image after each log entry. It provides auto-refreshing visual summaries of your calorie data, making it easy to track your daily consumption at a glance. The tool is designed for personal use and runs entirely on your local machine.
