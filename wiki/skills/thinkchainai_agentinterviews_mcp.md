> Conduct AI-powered qualitative research interviews and surveys at scale with [Agent Interviews](https://agentinterviews.com). Create interviewers, manage research projects, recruit participants, and analyze interview data through MCP.

Agent Interviews MCP is a server that enables conducting AI-powered qualitative research interviews and surveys at scale. It allows users to create virtual interviewers, manage research projects, recruit participants, and analyze interview data through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This tool automates the process of qualitative data collection, making it easier to gather insights from large numbers of participants.
