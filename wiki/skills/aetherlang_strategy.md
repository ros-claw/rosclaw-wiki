> > Game theory, Monte Carlo simulations, behavioral economics, and competitive war gaming.

Aetherlang-strategy is a project that applies game theory and Monte Carlo simulations to model strategic decision-making. It integrates insights from behavioral economics to simulate human biases and competitive war gaming scenarios. The tool enables analysis of outcomes under various strategies and uncertainty. It is designed for researchers and practitioners interested in simulation-based strategy development.
