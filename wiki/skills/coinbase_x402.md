coinbase/x402 is a TypeScript library that implements HTTP 402 Payment Required for AI services. It enables pay-per-use access by requiring micropayments before serving responses, integrating with Coinbase wallet. The protocol is compatible with OpenAI-style APIs and targets a decentralized AI economy. It is listed under Coinbase's sponsorship of TypeScript tools.
