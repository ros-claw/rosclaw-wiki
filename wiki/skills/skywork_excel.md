> AI-powered spreadsheet operations for creating, analyzing and generating reports.

Skywork-Excel is an AI-powered tool designed to automate spreadsheet operations such as creating, analyzing, and generating reports. It leverages artificial intelligence to streamline tasks typically done manually in spreadsheet software. Users can interact with data more efficiently, reducing the time spent on data manipulation and report generation. The tool aims to enhance productivity by providing intelligent suggestions and automation for common spreadsheet workflows.
