> A principled, spec-driven workflow that transforms large problems into focused, incremental missions using Claude Code's specialized sub agents. Includes slash-commands, sub agents, and specialized workflows designed for specific parts of the SDLC.

The AB Method is a principled, specification-driven workflow that helps break large problems into smaller, focused missions. It leverages Claude Code's specialized sub-agents to perform incremental tasks. The method includes slash-commands and sub-agents for various parts of the software development lifecycle (SDLC). This approach aims to improve productivity and maintain focus on specific goals.
