> Look up contacts from macOS Contacts.app.

A utility for querying and retrieving contact information from the macOS Contacts application. It provides a command-line interface to look up contacts by name or other criteria, enabling automation and scripting. The tool integrates directly with Apple's Contacts database, offering a simple way to access contact data programmatically.
