> A platform that evaluates LLMs by having them fight in Street Fighter III, testing speed, adaptability, and real-time decision-making as proxies for harness responsiveness under tight latency constraints.

The LLM Colosseum Leaderboard is an innovative benchmark that evaluates large language models through battles in the video game Street Fighter III. It measures speed, adaptability, and real-time decision-making as proxies for responsiveness under tight latency constraints. This approach provides a dynamic test of LLM performance beyond traditional static benchmarks.
