> Bitcoin Lightning payment CLI for agents.

Bitkit-cli is a command-line tool designed for Bitcoin Lightning payments. It enables agents (likely AI or automated systems) to easily make payments over the Lightning Network. The tool provides a simple interface for initiating and managing lightning transactions. It is intended to integrate with agent-based systems for seamless micropayments.
