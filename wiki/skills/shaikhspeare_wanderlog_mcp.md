This MCP server allows AI assistants to interact with Wanderlog, a travel planning platform, to read and modify trip itineraries. Users can manage places, hotels, notes, checklists, and dates through natural language commands. It automates itinerary management for both travelers and agents, integrating AI into the travel and transportation domain.
