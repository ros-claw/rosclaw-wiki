> A benchmark for evaluating LLM agents on tool use and complex planning within multiple constraints, revealing how harness design handles multi-constraint satisfaction and long-horizon planning.

TravelPlanner is a benchmark designed to evaluate LLM agents on their ability to use tools and perform complex planning under multiple constraints. It tests how well agent harness designs handle multi-constraint satisfaction and long-horizon planning tasks. The benchmark reveals strengths and weaknesses in current LLM-based planning systems, particularly in managing trade-offs between different constraints.
