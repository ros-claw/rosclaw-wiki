Pit different LLMs against each other to explore ideas from diverse perspectives.

Brainstorm-mcp is an MCP server that orchestrates multi-round brainstorming debates between different large language models, such as GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq, and Ollama. It lets users pit AI models against each other to explore ideas from diverse perspectives, supporting creative problem-solving and idea generation. The tool is cross-platform and integrates with the Model Context Protocol ecosystem to enable structured, adversarial or collaborative discussions among multiple AI agents.
