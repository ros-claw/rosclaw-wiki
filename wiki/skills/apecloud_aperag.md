> 🐍 ☁️ 🏠 - Production-ready RAG platform combining Graph RAG, vector search, and full-text search. Best choice for building your own Knowledge Graph and for Context Engineering

ApeRAG is a production-ready RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) platform that integrates Graph RAG, vector search, and full-text search. It is designed for building customizable knowledge graphs and enabling context engineering. The platform is suitable for applications requiring hybrid search capabilities and semantic understanding. It provides a robust foundation for implementing knowledge-intensive AI systems.
