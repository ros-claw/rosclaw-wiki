> Guides agents through opening.

Elevenlabs-open-account is a tool that guides AI agents through the process of opening an account on ElevenLabs. It automates the steps needed to register and set up an account, allowing agents to easily access ElevenLabs' text-to-speech services. This tool is designed for use within AI agent frameworks that require programmatic account creation, streamlining the onboarding process for agents needing to interact with ElevenLabs.
