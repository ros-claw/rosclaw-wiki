> Adversarial analysis to critique, fix.

Adversarial prompting involves crafting inputs designed to exploit weaknesses in AI models, particularly large language models, to expose vulnerabilities. This technique is used to critique model behavior and then apply fixes to improve robustness and safety. By simulating attacks, developers can identify and patch flaws before deployment. It is a critical part of red-teaming and security evaluation for AI systems.
