> Play Blinko (on-chain Plinko) headlessly on Abstract chain.

Blinko is a headless client for playing an on-chain version of Plinko on the Abstract blockchain. It allows users to automate gameplay without a graphical interface. The project is designed for seamless interaction with the blockchain-based Plinko game.
