The MyMedi-AI MCP server provides healthcare billing and administrative tools for AI agents, including lookup of 81,769 medical codes (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS) with RVU and OPPS pricing. It also offers prior authorization prediction, medical named entity recognition (NER), claims validation, denial-risk scoring, HIPAA compliance auditing, and NPI/drug enrichment. The server has 20 tools and operates on a pay-per-call model with 10 free credits.
