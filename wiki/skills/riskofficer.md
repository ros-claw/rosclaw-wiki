> Manage investment portfolios, calculate risk metrics.

riskofficer is a tool designed for managing investment portfolios and calculating key risk metrics. It helps users assess portfolio risk through measures like value at risk and standard deviation. The tool supports portfolio optimization and provides reporting capabilities. It is intended for use in financial analysis and risk management contexts.
