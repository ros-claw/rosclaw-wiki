This project provides a TypeScript implementation of Porter's Five Forces framework, enabling developers to model and analyze competitive forces within an industry. It offers data structures and functions to evaluate threat of new entrants, bargaining power of buyers/suppliers, threat of substitutes, and industry rivalry. The library is designed for integration into business intelligence or strategy applications, allowing programmatic assessment of market attractiveness.
