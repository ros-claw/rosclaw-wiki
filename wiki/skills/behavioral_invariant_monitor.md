> Helps verify that AI agent skills maintain consistent behavioral invariants across repeated executions — detecting.

The behavioral-invariant-monitor is a tool designed to ensure that AI agent skills maintain consistent behavioral invariants across repeated executions. It detects deviations from expected patterns, helping to verify reliability and reproducibility in agent behavior. This is particularly useful for debugging and validating AI systems in dynamic environments.
