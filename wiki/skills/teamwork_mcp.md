> 🎖️ 🏎️ ☁️ 🍎 🪟 🐧 - Project and resource management platform that keeps your client projects on track, makes managing resources a breeze, and keeps your profits on point.

This MCP server integrates Teamwork.com's project and resource management platform, enabling AI assistants to interact with projects, tasks, and resources. It allows users to manage client projects, track progress, and monitor profitability through natural language commands. The server provides tools for resource allocation, time tracking, and project reporting. It is designed for teams using Teamwork to streamline their workflows and keep projects on track.
