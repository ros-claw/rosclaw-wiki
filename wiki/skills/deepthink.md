> DeepThink is the user's personal knowledge base.

DeepThink is a personal knowledge base that helps users store, organize, and retrieve their personal notes, ideas, and resources. It aims to function as a 'second brain' for efficient knowledge management. The tool likely incorporates AI features to enhance search and organization. Users can build a structured repository of their knowledge over time.
