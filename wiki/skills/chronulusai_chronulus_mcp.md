Chronulus MCP is a server that connects Chronulus AI's forecasting and prediction models to the Model Context Protocol (MCP), enabling AI agents and tools to perform time series forecasting and predictive analytics. It is listed under Data Science Tools, indicating its role in data-driven decision-making. The integration allows users to predict outcomes across various domains through a standardized interface, simplifying access to advanced AI forecasting capabilities.
