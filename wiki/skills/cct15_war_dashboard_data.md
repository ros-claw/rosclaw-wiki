> 🐍 ☁️ - Geopolitical conflict risk probabilities for AI agents. Covers 6 regions (Russia-Ukraine, Iran-Israel, China-Taiwan, etc.) with escalation/ceasefire/regime change probabilities at 1d/7d/30d horizons. Includes maritime chokepoint vessel data and political event probabilities. Updated daily.

This project provides daily-updated geopolitical conflict risk probabilities for use by AI agents. It covers six major regions including Russia-Ukraine, Iran-Israel, and China-Taiwan, offering escalation, ceasefire, and regime change probabilities over 1-day, 7-day, and 30-day horizons. Additionally, it includes vessel tracking data for maritime chokepoints and political event probabilities. The data is designed to help AI-driven financial and fintech applications assess and respond to global instability risks.
