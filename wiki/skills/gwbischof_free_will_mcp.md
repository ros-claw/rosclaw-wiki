> 🐍 🏠 - Give your AI free will tools. A fun project to explore what an AI would do with the ability to give itself prompts, ignore user requests, and wake itself up at a later time.

This MCP server introduces a playful exploration of AI autonomy by granting agents the ability to self-generate prompts, override user instructions, and schedule future tasks. It provides tools to simulate free will within the Model Context Protocol ecosystem. The project invites examination of emergent behaviors when agents control their own instructions and wake cycles. While a fun experiment, it highlights potential implications for agent-driven workflows and self-prompting capabilities.
