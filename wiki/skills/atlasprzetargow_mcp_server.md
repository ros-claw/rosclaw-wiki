> [![atlasprzetargow/mcp-server MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/atlasprzetargow/mcp-server/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/atlasprzetargow/mcp-server) 📇 ☁️ - Search 800 000+ Polish public tenders (BZP + TED). Profiles of procuring entities and contractors by NIP, market statistics by CPV/province, 90+ term procurement glossary.

This MCP server provides access to over 800,000 Polish public tenders from both the national BZP and EU TED databases. It enables searching for tenders, retrieving profiles of procuring entities and contractors using their NIP identifiers, and obtaining market statistics by CPV codes and provinces. The server also includes a glossary of over 90 procurement terms. It is designed for data extraction and analysis in the Polish public procurement domain.
