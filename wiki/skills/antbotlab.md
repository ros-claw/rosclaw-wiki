AntBotLab is an open-source project focused on developing autonomous ant-like robots that exhibit swarm intelligence behaviors. It provides tools and simulations for studying collective robotics, inspired by the coordination and navigation strategies of ants. The project aims to advance embodied intelligence by enabling robots to collaborate and adapt in complex environments.
