> Meta-skill for pre-accounting automation by orchestrating gmail, deepread-ocr, stripe-api, and xero.

Bookkeeper is a meta-skill that automates pre-accounting tasks by integrating Gmail, DeepRead OCR, Stripe API, and Xero. It orchestrates these services to streamline financial data processing, reduce manual entry, and improve accounting accuracy. The tool helps users manage email-based transactions, invoice OCR, payment processing, and synchronization with accounting software.
