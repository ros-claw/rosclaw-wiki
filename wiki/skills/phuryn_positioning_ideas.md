A TypeScript library for organizing and positioning ideas on a grid or priority matrix. It enables users to assign coordinates, categories, and weights to ideas for easier comparison and decision-making. The tool is designed to support brainstorming sessions and structured thinking workflows. It is built with TypeScript to provide type safety and developer-friendly APIs.
