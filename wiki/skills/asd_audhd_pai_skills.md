> New collection, first skill [pda-reframing](https://github.com/emory/ASD-AuDHD-PAI-Skills/blob/main/Skills/pda-reframing/SKILL.md) can reframe requests or decisions to defeat Persistent Demand Avoidance flavors of autism spectrum disorders, or people with ADHD that struggle to Start tasks and need help aligning with a task.

This project is a collection of assistive technology skills designed for individuals with autism and ADHD. The first skill, pda-reframing, helps reframe requests or decisions to overcome Persistent Demand Avoidance, a common challenge in autism spectrum disorders. It also aids people with ADHD who struggle to start tasks by aligning them with the task at hand. The collection is listed under Skills > Assistive Technology and aims to improve daily functioning and task engagement.
