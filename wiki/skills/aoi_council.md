> AOI Council — multi-perspective decision synthesis templates (public-safe).

AOI Council provides a set of templates for synthesizing decisions from multiple perspectives, designed for public-safe applications. It helps structure deliberation among AI agents or human participants to reach consensus or identify trade-offs. The project emphasizes transparency and safety in decision-making processes. These templates can be used to facilitate group discussions or automated reasoning systems.
