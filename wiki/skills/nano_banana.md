> Google Gemini image generation plugin. Text-to-image, text-guided image editing, style transfer, 4K output, search grounding, and multi-reference composition — all from a single `/genimage` command. Powered by `gemini-2.5-flash-image` and `gemini-3-pro-image-preview`.

nano-banana is a plugin that integrates Google Gemini models for image generation. It supports text-to-image creation, text-guided image editing, style transfer, and 4K resolution output. The plugin also offers search grounding and multi-reference composition through a single `/genimage` command. It leverages the capabilities of the `gemini-2.5-flash-image` and `gemini-3-pro-image-preview` models.
