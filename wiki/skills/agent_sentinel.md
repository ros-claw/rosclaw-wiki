> The operational circuit breaker for this agent.

Agent-sentinel is a tool that implements the circuit breaker pattern for AI agents. It monitors agent operations and automatically halts execution when failures or errors exceed a threshold, preventing cascading failures. This helps maintain system stability and reliability in agent-based workflows.
