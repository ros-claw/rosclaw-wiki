> Creates product requirement plans by reading PRP methodology, following template structure, creating comprehensive requirements, and structuring product definitions for development.

The /create-prp command automates the creation of product requirement plans (PRPs) by reading a specific methodology, following a template, and generating comprehensive requirements. It structures product definitions to guide development teams, ensuring consistency and completeness. This tool is part of a larger set of slash commands for project and task management.
