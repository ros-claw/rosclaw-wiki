> Discover which AI prompts and topics matter for a brand's Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) using only free tools.

This project, 'aeo-prompt-research-free,' focuses on Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) by helping brands identify which AI prompts and topics are most relevant for their visibility. It provides methods using only free tools, making AEO accessible without paid subscriptions. The goal is to understand how AI systems perceive and respond to brand-related queries, enabling better content strategy. It bridges AI prompt research with search engine optimization for brand awareness.
