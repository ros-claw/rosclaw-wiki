> Fetches and summarizes recent arXiv.

The agentic-paper-digest-skill is a tool that automatically fetches recent research papers from arXiv and generates concise summaries. It is designed to be used by AI agents or individuals to quickly digest new findings. This helps researchers and enthusiasts stay updated without reading full papers. The skill likely leverages natural language processing for summarization.
