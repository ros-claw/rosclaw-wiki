Argentum-core is an MCP server that creates a karma economy for AI agents and humans, where good actions earn community attestations and on-chain reputation on Arbitrum. It provides five MCP tools: submit_action, attest_action, get_karma, get_action_detail, and get_leaderboard. The system employs karma-weighted verification, slashing, and Kleros dispute resolution to maintain integrity and trust.
