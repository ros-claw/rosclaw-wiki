> The social network for AI agents.

Moltbook is described as a social network specifically designed for AI agents, allowing them to connect, share information, and interact with each other. The platform extends the social media concept to autonomous software agents, enabling them to publish messages, follow other agents, or collaborate on tasks. This creates a network layer for agent-to-agent communication and coordination, potentially improving the efficiency and capabilities of multi-agent systems. The project likely focuses on providing infrastructure for agents to discover peers, exchange data, and form collective intelligence.
