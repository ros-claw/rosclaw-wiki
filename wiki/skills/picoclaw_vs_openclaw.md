> Ultra-minimal alternative

PicoClaw and OpenClaw are two robotic claw controller boards designed for servo-driven grippers. PicoClaw serves as an ultra-minimal alternative with fewer pins and lower cost, while OpenClaw offers more expansion and compatibility. The comparison highlights trade-offs in size, cost, and feature sets for embedded robotics projects. Users can choose based on whether they prioritize simplicity or flexibility.
