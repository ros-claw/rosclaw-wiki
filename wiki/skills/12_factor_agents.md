> HumanLayer's operating principles for production agents, including explicit prompts, state ownership, and clean pause-resume behavior.

The 12 Factor Agents outlines a set of operating principles for building production-ready AI agents. It emphasizes the importance of explicit prompts, clear state ownership, and clean pause-resume behavior to ensure reliability and maintainability. These principles are intended to guide developers in designing robust agent workflows that can be safely managed in real-world applications.
