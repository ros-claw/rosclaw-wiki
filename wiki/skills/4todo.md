> Manage 4todo (4to.do) from chat.

4todo is a tool that allows users to manage their tasks on the 4to.do platform directly from a chat interface. It integrates with chat systems to enable creating, updating, and viewing tasks without leaving the conversation. This enhances productivity by streamlining task management within communication workflows. The project targets users who prefer chat-based interactions for managing their to-do lists.
