> 🐍 🏠 - Query TrainingPeaks workouts, analyze CTL/ATL/TSB fitness metrics, and compare power/pace PRs through Claude Desktop.

This project is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that connects to TrainingPeaks, a popular training platform for athletes. It allows users to query workout data, analyze fitness metrics such as Chronic Training Load (CTL), Acute Training Load (ATL), and Training Stress Balance (TSB), and compare power or pace personal records (PRs). The server integrates with Claude Desktop, enabling conversational interaction with training data. It is designed for athletes and coaches who want to use AI to explore and understand their performance trends.
