> Automate Segment: sources, destinations, tracking, and warehouse connections.

Segment Automation is a tool under Composio's app automation suite that enables automated management of Segment configurations. It handles tasks such as setting up sources, configuring destinations, managing tracking events, and connecting data warehouses. This streamlines data pipeline operations by reducing manual setup efforts.
