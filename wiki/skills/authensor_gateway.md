> Fail-safe policy gate for OpenClaw marketplace skills.

authensor-gateway is a fail-safe policy gateway designed for the OpenClaw marketplace skills. It ensures that only authorized actions are allowed, providing a secure layer for skill execution. This system acts as a gatekeeper, verifying policies before any marketplace skill can run. Its design emphasizes safety and reliability, preventing unauthorized or harmful operations within the OpenClaw ecosystem.
