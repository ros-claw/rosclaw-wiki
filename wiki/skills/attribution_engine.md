> Helps creators clearly credit collaborators, tools.

The attribution-engine project is a tool designed to help creators properly credit their collaborators and tools used in their work. It aims to simplify the process of giving appropriate attribution, which is important in creative fields and especially relevant for AI-generated content. The engine likely provides a systematic way to track and display credits, ensuring transparency and recognition for all contributors.
