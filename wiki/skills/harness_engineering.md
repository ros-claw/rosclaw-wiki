> Thoughtworks' framing of harness work into context engineering, architectural constraints, and "garbage collection" against entropy.

Harness Engineering is a framework from Thoughtworks that organizes engineering work into three key areas: context engineering, which involves understanding the environment and constraints; architectural constraints, which define boundaries and principles; and garbage collection, which addresses entropy and technical debt accumulation. The concept aims to provide a structured approach to managing complexity and maintaining software systems. It helps teams focus on proactive, systematic practices rather than reactive firefighting. This framing is useful for aligning engineering efforts with long-term system health and adaptability.
