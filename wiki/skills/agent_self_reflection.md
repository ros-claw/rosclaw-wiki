> Periodic self-reflection on recent sessions.

This project implements periodic self-reflection for AI agents, allowing them to review and learn from recent sessions. It enables agents to analyze their own actions, decisions, and outcomes to improve future performance. The goal is to imbue agents with a form of meta-cognition that enhances autonomy and adaptability. By reflecting on past interactions, agents can identify patterns, correct errors, and refine their strategies over time.
