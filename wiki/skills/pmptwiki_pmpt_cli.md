> [![pmpt-mcp MCP server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@pmptwiki/pmpt-mcp/badges/score.svg)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@pmptwiki/pmpt-mcp) 📇 🏠 - Record and share your AI-driven development journey. Auto-saves project milestones with summaries, generates structured AI prompts via 5-question planning, and publishes to [pmptwiki.com](https://pmptwiki.com) community platform.

This tool helps developers record and share their AI-driven development journeys. It automatically saves project milestones with summaries and uses a five-question planning process to generate structured AI prompts. Users can publish their work to the pmptwiki community platform for collaboration and sharing.
