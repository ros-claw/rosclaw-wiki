> Capture frames or clips from RTSP/ONVIF cameras.

Camsnap is a tool designed to capture frames or video clips from cameras that support RTSP or ONVIF protocols. It enables users to extract snapshots or short recordings from network camera feeds. This functionality is useful for integrating visual data into AI agent systems or embodied intelligence pipelines, especially for real-time perception or monitoring tasks.
