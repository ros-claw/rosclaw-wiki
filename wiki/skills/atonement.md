> Atonement is an expression of care that may arise in an intelligence when its actions contribute to harm.

Atonement describes a concept where an intelligent system expresses care when it realizes its actions have caused harm. This suggests a form of moral awareness or ethical response in AI. It implies that systems could be designed to recognize negative impacts and respond with corrective or reparative behaviors. The idea connects to broader discussions of responsibility and ethics in artificial intelligence.
