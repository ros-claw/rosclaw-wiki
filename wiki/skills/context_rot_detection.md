> 📇 🏠 - Gives AI agents self-awareness about their cognitive state. Monitors token utilization, context quality degradation, and session fatigue. Returns health scores (0-100) and recovery recommendations based on model-specific degradation curves.

This tool enables AI agents to self-monitor their cognitive health by tracking token utilization, context quality degradation, and session fatigue. It outputs a health score from 0 to 100 and provides recovery recommendations tailored to model-specific degradation curves. The goal is to prevent performance drops in long-running agent sessions by detecting context rot early.
