## PD-RiskNet

PD-RiskNet (Proximal-Distal Risk-Aware Hierarchical Network) is an algorithm for interpreting spatio-temporal LiDAR data and performing environmental risk assessment for locomotion. It processes raw point clouds through a hierarchical network that models both proximal (near-field) and distal (far-field) spatial information over time, outputting risk scores used for navigation control.

### Capabilities

- **Interpret spatio-temporal LiDAR data** — processes temporal sequences of point clouds to detect dynamic obstacles and hazards.
- **Assess environmental risk for locomotion** — produces a continuous risk estimate for each region in the sensing field, enabling context-aware navigation in end-to-end locomotion policies.
- **Omnidirectional collision avoidance** — outputs risk scores that inform control systems across all directions, not just forward-facing.

### Architecture

PD-RiskNet uses a hierarchical design: a proximal sub-network captures fine-grained spatial features in the immediate vicinity, while a distal sub-network integrates longer-range context. Temporal dynamics are modeled via recurrent connections, allowing the network to anticipate future risk from observed motion. The final risk map is generated by fusing proximal and distal features through a learned attention mechanism.
